## What applies to our landing page

1. **One avatar first (Hormozi, focus).** The page now speaks to kids, teens, adults, families, new Muslims and four
   tracks at once. Lead with one person: the Muslim parent in the West with a child of about 6–12 who wants Quran.
   Every other audience gets one clear door lower down, not equal weight up top.
2. **Proof over promise (Hormozi, trust).** Almost every block today is a promise ("your teacher stays", "a note after
   every lesson"). Show the thing instead: a real after-lesson note, a real teacher profile, a short real lesson
   moment (with consent), the booking screen. Shotcraft's idea fits exactly: our own portal screens as the film.
3. **Ask, don't push (closer, reactance → self-persuasion).** Replace claim-heavy copy with 2–3 questions the parent
   answers: what they hope changes for their child, how long it has been like this, what happens if a year passes the
   same. Reflect their answer back with a matching path and the free lesson. Gentle tone only: no fear or guilt (our
   rules), the parent names the cost, we never state it for them.
4. **An offer that is hard to refuse (Hormozi, offer + free start).** Stack what we already give into one clear offer
   next to the button: free 30-minute lesson, no card, the same teacher every time, free switch if the fit is wrong,
   a note after every lesson, sit in on any lesson. This is risk reversal, said once, plainly.
5. **Start smaller than the fear (hand-drawn explainer).** The first step must look tiny: "30 minutes, no card". The
   steps section should feel this way.
6. **One message from ad to page (Hormozi, unify).** The hero headline should repeat the words of the ad or post that
   brought the visitor, so the click feels continued, not restarted.
7. **Use parents' words (Hormozi, talk to customers).** Headlines and FAQ from what families actually say on WhatsApp
   and in the trial, not from our vocabulary.
8. **Remind, don't teach (Hormozi).** Fewer explanatory sections; repeat the few things that matter (one teacher,
   a note after each lesson, free first lesson) at the top, middle and end.

## What applies to the intro film

- **Hook in the first second:** open on a kinetic Arabic word (e.g. زاد) with Mask Reveal, then Zoom Through its
  letter into scene two. Not a calm empty bag.
- **Kinetic type tied to the voice:** one word or short phrase on screen at a time, one word highlighted in crimson,
  Stagger at ~0.05 s, Whip Pan with motion blur between scenes, Slice for the turn ("but…").
- **Proof inside the film:** real portal screens (after-lesson note, teacher profile, booking) moved with camera
  pushes, Shotcraft style, instead of drawn icons.
- **Finish:** film grain, smooth easing, animated captions, chapter bar if longer than 30 s; Zad appears (owner rule).
- Tools to evaluate before building (not installed): Bang Motion, Video Shotcraft, the Remotion skill. We already
  have Remotion in `marketing/promo-video`.

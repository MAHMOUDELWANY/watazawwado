# The owner's taste (started 2026-11-21)

What the owner picked and rejected, in his words and in design terms. He is not a designer: every entry names the
term and explains it plainly, so "I like this one" can be turned into a rule. Read before any prototype; add an
entry after every pick. A pattern seen three times moves into the `WATAZAWWADO-design-system` skill.

| Date | Shown | Picked | Rejected | Term (plain meaning) | Rule we take |

# Classroom audit after the owner's first real lesson, 2026-11-20

Lesson used: booking `18bcf800` (Noor Khan, Fusha trial, 19 Nov 09:30–10:00 UTC), pilot mode on. Sources: the owner's
notes, the attendance events of that booking in production, and the code on `main` (c751e7af).

**Why our tests missed most of this.** The E2E run used a local server with fake camera and mic. The local server does
not send the production headers from `vercel.json`, and the child phone was never part of a scenario. From now on, every
classroom change is checked on a Vercel preview with a real camera and mic, and with the child phone.

## A. Bugs with a confirmed root cause

| # | What the owner saw | Root cause (confirmed) | Fix size |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Mic and camera "blocked by the browser". Retry never asked for permission. Mic button did nothing. | `vercel.json` sends `Permissions-Policy: camera=()`, which forbids the camera on the whole site. The room asks for camera and mic together, so both fail, and the browser never shows a prompt. | one line + a test |
| A2 | The 720p / network check never ran. | It needs the camera (A1). The room also let the teacher in with no media, without a clear "fix your camera" screen. | small |
| A3 | The child's own phone (linked with the code) saw the lesson but got "room not available". | `server/routes/childDevices.ts` lets a child phone call only listed routes. The classroom routes (`/api/student/classroom/*`, `/api/classroom/*`) are missing, so the gate fell back to the old slides page, which also failed. | small |
| A4 | Teacher was 10 min 51 s late. No reason popup appeared. | `recordTeacherLateness` looks up `room:<id>:teacher:joined`. The teacher's join is stored as `room:<id>:teacher:joined:<joinId>`, so it never matches and no late-reason row is created. (Also: a camera blocked by the browser counts as a "teacher-side incident", which would wrongly excuse lateness.) | small |
| A5 | PDF: the teacher cannot scroll. | The drawing layer sits `absolute inset-0` over the scroll box and catches the wheel and touch. It also does not move with the page, so marks drift when you scroll. | medium |
| A6 | The slides picker opened a curriculum that is not the learner's. | `parts/Curriculum.tsx` opens the first subject in the catalog. It ignores the booking's service and the learner's progress. | medium |
| A7 | "Waiting for <name>" stayed on. | The student kept joining and leaving every 1–5 minutes (7 joins in 16 min). Presence follows the media connection, so with no media (A1) and those rejoins the label stayed. To check after A1/A3. | to check |

## C. Design

The owner finds the room cheap, not premium, and not our identity. The 2026-11-18 direction (espresso stage, glass
controls) did not land. Per our standing rule, 2–3 prototype looks with our palette (crimson, teal, copper, sand, gold;
espresso for dark; no green), our type and our arch, before any rebuild.

## D. What worked

Room window (opens 10 min early), the guardian is shown to the teacher and hidden from the child, silent admin
observation, screen share (and it survived Wi-Fi to data), the timer, the focus warning, and PDF upload seen by both.

## E. Proposed order (waiting for the owner)

1. **Fix now (A1–A6, B2, B7):** so the owner can teach 2–3 real students in the room this week, with Zoom as backup.
2. **After the lesson (B3, B4):** auto-complete by attendance, report draft, files in the report, rating.
3. **Design (C) + own board (B6) + prep mode (B1):** prototypes first, owner picks, then build.
4. Release 2 items (B5 and the rest of the spec list).

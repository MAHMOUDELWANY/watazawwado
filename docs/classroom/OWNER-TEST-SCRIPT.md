# Classroom: the owner's full test, from booking to report (2026-11-20)

Needs: the pilot teacher's account on a laptop with Chrome, a parent account, and a phone for the child (linked by code).
Book a lesson with the pilot teacher for in about 30 minutes.

1. **Prep (teacher, any time before):** Today -> "Prepare lesson". The slides open on the booked subject (lesson 1 if
   it is the learner's first lesson). Upload a PDF, write a note. "Join the room" unlocks 10 minutes before.
2. **Parent PIN (parent):** Family tab -> set a 4-digit PIN. Try "Forgot the PIN?" (the code arrives by email).
   "Hand this device to my child" -> only the child's pages remain; "Back to parent" asks for the PIN.
3. **Waiting room (both):** the browser asks for the mic, then the camera. Refuse the camera once: the screen explains how
   to allow it, and the mic still works. Speaker test, mic meter, network test.
4. **In the lesson (teacher late by 3+ minutes on purpose):**
   - Video and sound both ways. Talk-time ratio visible to the teacher only.
   - The slides open where prep left them. Pen and laser on a slide.
   - Board: pen, shapes, Arabic text with the tashkeel keyboard; let the learner draw.
   - PDF from prep: scroll with the mouse wheel, write on it; the marks stay on the words.
   - Tools: a correction card, an understanding check, the stage bar, a YouTube link, an audio file, shadowing, one
     practice game (fill the blanks), learner comfort (larger text) on the learner's side.
   - Mushaf tab (Quran/Fusha/Islamic lessons): turn pages, mark two words.
   - Screen share; switch tab (the learner's video floats); background blur on.
   - Parent: "Watch the lesson" asks for the PIN; the teacher sees "guardian watching", the child does not.
   - Admin: watch silently from the admin account.
   - Switch the child's phone from Wi-Fi to data; leave and rejoin: the same slide, board, tools and mushaf page return.
5. **End 5 minutes early:** the teacher must write why.
6. **After:** the teacher's dashboard asks for the late reason (blocking). The report opens filled in: pages covered,
   files with their names, corrections, checks, talk time, mushaf marks, early-end reason. Edit and send.
7. **Family side:** the lesson shows "completed" by itself, the files and the report, the rating prompt, the corrections in
   the daily review, mushaf marks in the Quran tab.
8. **Admin:** the lesson page (booking window -> lesson view) shows the timeline, attendance verdict, late and early
   reasons, quality, observations, rating. Late reasons page: excuse or deduct.

Send what fails (a screenshot and the step number); every finding goes into `docs/classroom/AUDIT-2026-11-20.md`.

# Content Rules (binding)

Every post, video, caption, thumbnail, carousel, email and comment reply published for Watazawwado follows these rules.
They apply the root `RULES.md` (§2b subjects, §3 prices, §4 honesty, §6/6b voice and mission) to social media. If they
disagree with the root RULES.md, the root wins. Status as in the root file; new owner decisions are added here with a
date.

## 1. Religious accuracy (never negotiable)

1. **Quran text** is copied from a verified source (the Quran MCP server `fetch_quran`, or quran.com), never typed from
   memory. Show surah name and ayah number. Translations name their source (e.g. Saheeh International).
2. **Hadith**: only authentic (sahih/hasan) with the collection and number; if unsure, leave it out.
3. **Recitation on camera** is checked before publishing: a teacher re-listens to every recited word. A Tajweed video
   with a Tajweed mistake is deleted, not explained. We never recite the Quran wrongly on purpose, not even to
   demonstrate a mistake: "wrong vs right" examples use isolated letters or practice syllables.
4. **No fatwas.** Fiqh questions in comments get: "Great question, ask your teacher in class or a scholar you trust."
   Teaching follows the moderate Azhari foundation, Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah (root RULES.md §6).
5. **No images of prophets, companions or angels**, not even silhouettes with faces. Stories use places, objects,
   hands, landscapes, calligraphy, or our own curriculum art.
6. **Sound**: no musical instruments. Voice, natural sound, our CC0 sound effects or a soft drone only. Never put any
   sound over recitation.
7. **Respect**: no jokes about religion, no trending audio with music, no trends that mock other Muslims or groups.
   Disagreements between scholars are not content.

## 2. Voice

1. **The academy speaks** ("we", "our team", "our teachers"). A teacher on camera introduces themselves as a teacher
   of Watazawwado ("I'm Ustadh Mahmoud, one of the teachers at Watazawwado"). The channel is never a personal
   channel. (root RULES.md §6b, CLAUDE.md)
2. Warm, wise, close, never salesy; no hype words ("amazing", "life-changing", "best academy"); short sentences.
3. Arabic copy: the 50/50 style of root RULES.md §6 (simple Fusha with a warm Egyptian touch).
4. Every caption and script goes through the `humanizer` skill before publishing.

## 3. Claims and proof

1. **Never invent**: reviews, student numbers, results, credentials, "certified" badges, before/after stories.
2. Prices only as in root RULES.md §3: $9/hour Quran, Tajweed, Zad Path; from $13/hour Arabic and English; "from"
   prices; free trial 30 minutes (max 45), one per new student.
3. No fake urgency or scarcity ("only 3 spots left!") unless it is literally true and the owner approved it.
4. No promises of outcomes ("memorize a Juz in 30 days"). Say what we do, not what the child will become.
5. Teachers' backgrounds only as written on the About page; no degree claims. Say plainly where each teacher is
   from when asked (false "Arab teacher" claims are a common complaint in this market).
6. **Reviews (US FTC rule 2024, UK DMCC Act 2025).** Never write, buy or post fake, AI-made or staff/relative reviews
   without disclosure; never tie any gift, discount or benefit to a review or to a positive rating; any thank-you
   goes to every reviewer whatever they wrote; never hide or cherry-pick bad reviews; reply to every review kindly,
   quickly and without defensiveness.

## 4. Children, privacy and safety

1. **Never show a student's face, name, voice or screen** without written consent from the parent (for a child) or the
   learner (for an adult). Even with consent, children are not named and their faces are not shown; voice or hands
   only.
2. Demo data only on screen (portal, reports, chat); never real accounts, emails or booking references.
3. **[Decided 2026-10-19] Only Ustadh Mahmoud appears in content.** Ustadha Afnan does not appear (no face, voice,
   hands or photo in videos); we may say in words that families can choose a female teacher. Any future teacher
   appears only with their own written agreement.
4. DMs with minors: never. A child who writes in DMs gets a polite reply asking a parent to contact us on WhatsApp.
5. Only the official contact number (root RULES.md §6: WhatsApp +48 508 157 886). No other number anywhere.

## 5. Competitors

Never name, show or mock another academy, teacher or app. We talk about what parents should look for; viewers can
compare on their own.

## 6. Links and tracking

1. One link in each bio: `https://watazawwado.academy/?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio`
   (`instagram`, `youtube`, `tiktok`, `facebook`, `telegram`).
2. Video descriptions and posts: `utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<series-slug>`. Never put personal data in a URL.
3. One call to action per post (PLAN.md §7).

## 7. Curriculum on screen

Only lessons the owner has marked **reviewed** may appear publicly, and never whole lessons: a few pages or a game
moment, enough to show the quality. Curriculum content itself is still owned by the curriculum process
(`docs/curriculum/RULES.md`).

## 8. Before anything is published (checklist)

- [ ] Quran/hadith verified and sourced (§1)
- [ ] Recitation re-listened by a teacher (§1.3)
- [ ] No music, no forbidden images (§1.5–1.6)
- [ ] Academy voice, humanized, short (§2)
- [ ] No invented claims; prices exact (§3)
- [ ] No child's face/name; demo data only (§4)
- [ ] Captions burned in, readable on a phone, inside the safe zone (`PRODUCTION.md` §4)
- [ ] One CTA; UTM link (§6)
- [ ] Logged in `LOG.md`

## 3. Who we talk to

1. **Primary: the parent (usually the mother), 28–45, in the US/UK/Canada/Australia/Europe.** Busy, wants a safe,
   structured, consistent teacher for a 6–14-year-old; worries the child finds Quran class boring; wants to see
   progress. Speaks English; may or may not read Arabic. *She books the trial.*
2. **Arab diaspora parents** (Egypt, Levant, Gulf, North Africa) whose children are losing Arabic. Speak Arabic,
   watch Arabic content, care about Egyptian/Fusha Arabic and Quran for the kids.
3. **Adult learners and reverts** (18–40): Quran reading from zero, Tajweed, Arabic. Search-driven on YouTube.

Language split [Proposed]: **70% English** (audiences 1 and 3), **30% Arabic** (audience 2). English videos get Arabic
captions when cheap; Arabic videos get English captions.

## 6. The six months

Dates of Islamic months are approximate and must be confirmed nearer the time. Ramadan 1448 is expected around

### Month 4 (Feb 2027): Ramadan

## 7. The path from a video to a booking

1. **See** (Short/Reel) → 2. **Trust** (profile, pinned posts, a long video, a teacher's face and voice) →
3. **Act**: the free trial on the site (sign up → onboarding → pick a teacher → trial) or a WhatsApp question.
Every post has **one** call to action. Discovery posts: follow / watch the full video. Trust and conversion posts:
"Book a free trial lesson" with the UTM link. Never more than one CTA in a post.

## 8. Measuring

Weekly (in `LOG.md`, 10 minutes): posts published, best and worst post with 3-second hold, average view duration,
saves + shares, profile → site clicks, **trial bookings** and where they came from.
Monthly: trials booked, trials → paid, cost (time), top 3 posts and why.

Starting targets [Proposed], recalibrated after week 6:
- Month 1: 4 posts a week every week (consistency is the target).
- Month 3: 10+ trial bookings in total since launch.
- Month 6: 15–30 trial bookings a month, 5+ real public reviews.

Lead source: the site does not yet ask "How did you hear about us?" and links need UTM tags. Adding a source question
at onboarding is an engineering task [Proposed], not part of this plan's content work.

## 10. Research notes (2026-10-19)

Competitors seen (public pages and Trustpilot summaries, not verified independently; never name them in public
content, `RULES.md` §5):
- Marketplaces (e.g. Qutor): pick-a-tutor model, short free trial, prices vary by tutor.
- Egypt/Pakistan-based academies (TarteeleQuran, Riwaq Al Quran, Al-Muhammadi, Quran Master Online, Online Qurann):
  monthly plans from about $16–40 for 2 sessions a week; many self-ranking "top 10" blogs; recurring review topics are
  teacher changes and communication.
- Content-only brands (Bayyinah, Madinah Arabic, Arabic YouTube channels): huge free content, no personal teacher.
- Gap we use: real teachers on camera + visible curriculum + clear prices + Western-family logistics + Egyptian Arabic
  for kids. Parent guides list "no plan, no visible progress, teacher talks all the time, child dreads class" as the
  signs a class is not working; our pillar E answers exactly those.

**One-line positioning [Proposed]:** "1-on-1 Quran, Islamic studies and Arabic for Western Muslim families, with your
own teacher and a curriculum you can actually see."
Arabic line: «حصص فردية مع معلّمك أنت، ومنهج تشوفه بعينك.»

**Added from the 2026-10-19 deep research** (`reports/Online Quran academy market.md`): price and the free trial do
not set us apart (every competitor has a trial; some charge $8/hour), so we never claim "cheapest" or "free trial" as
the edge. Bad reviews in this market are about operations (teacher changes, late teachers, missing invoices, pressure
to pay, false "Arab teacher" claims); praise is about a patient named teacher, a child who enjoys class and visible
progress. So we make **reliability visible**: the same named teacher, a sample parent report, prices and cancellation
terms on the page, where each teacher is from, and gentle correction on camera. The emotional core for parents is
the fear that a harsh, rote class turns the child away from the Quran.

**Our honest weaknesses** (the plan must cover them): no social proof yet, a small team, a new name, an owner who films
alone. Answers: show the product instead of claims (§2), founding-families feedback loop (§6, M3), consistency over
volume (§5).

## 3. Who we talk to

1. **Primary: the parent (usually the mother), 28–45, in the US/UK/Canada/Australia/Europe.** Busy, wants a safe,
   structured, consistent teacher for a 6–14-year-old; worries the child finds Quran class boring; wants to see
   progress. Speaks English; may or may not read Arabic. *She books the trial.*
2. **Arab diaspora parents** (Egypt, Levant, Gulf, North Africa) whose children are losing Arabic. Speak Arabic,
   watch Arabic content, care about Egyptian/Fusha Arabic and Quran for the kids.
3. **Adult learners and reverts** (18–40): Quran reading from zero, Tajweed, Arabic. Search-driven on YouTube.

Language split [Decided 2026-10-19, delegated]: **70% English** (audiences 1 and 3), **30% Arabic** (audience 2). English videos get Arabic
captions when cheap; Arabic videos get English captions.

## 7. The path from a video to a booking

1. **See** (Short/Reel; the sample lesson's first seconds can be shown publicly as a teaser) → 2. **Trust**
(profile, pinned posts, a long video, the teacher's face and voice) → 3. **Free account** (the lead magnet, below) →
4. **Free trial** → 5. **Same-day parent report** and a booked weekly slot.

**Lead magnet [Decided 2026-10-19, delegated]: the free account itself.** Signing up already captures a verified
email and sends our Brevo emails, so no separate list or tool is needed. A free account unlocks the **free sample of
Zad Essentials Lesson 1** (opening pages, stopping on the mystery of the visitor; in `/student/curriculum` once the
owner marks Lesson 1 reviewed), the **placement level check** with its printable report, and the printables (Tajweed
chart, Juz 'Amma tracker, Seerah worksheet). Printables are also posted on the Telegram/WhatsApp channels with the
sign-up link, for people not ready to sign up. Why: value before the wall converts best (our public value is the
videos; the account is the next step), and level checks convert better than PDFs.

**The trial** (benchmark: about 50% of trials become paid on tutoring platforms): message the family before; spend
about 10 minutes teaching and the rest on level, goals and a plan; for under-12s a parent is present; end by booking
the weekly slot; send a written parent report the same day (level, three goals, weekly plan, the named teacher, one
link to book).
Every post has **one** call to action. Discovery posts: follow / watch the full video. Trust and conversion posts:
"Book a free trial lesson" with the UTM link. Never more than one CTA in a post.

## 8. Measuring

Weekly (in `LOG.md`, 10 minutes): posts published, best and worst post with 3-second hold, average view duration,
saves + shares, profile → site clicks, **trial bookings** and where they came from.
Monthly: trials booked, trials → paid, cost (time), top 3 posts and why.

Starting targets [Proposed], recalibrated after week 6:
- Month 1: 4 posts a week every week (consistency is the target).
- Month 3: 10+ trial bookings in total since launch.
- Month 6: 15–30 trial bookings a month, 5+ real public reviews.

**Funnel (added 2026-10-20 from the external research review):** each week also count level checks, trials
attended, paid families and families still learning 4 weeks later, plus the top repeated questions (Notion Weekly
Numbers). Views are diagnosis only. A **qualified lead**: the subject is offered, the age fits (6–17 in a family
account, or 18+) and a teacher has a slot in the family's time zone. Each post's link adds
`utm_content=id<calendar ID>`. After 4 weeks (review 29 Nov) apply the decision rules in the Notion review page: fix
the step where the funnel drops, change one variable at a time, never judge a platform on a handful of leads.
Interview the first 5–8 families after their trial (what made you book, what worried you, why not).

Lead source: links carry UTM tags (`RULES.md` §6). **Captured since 2026-10-20:** the first tagged visit's UTM tags and
referring site are kept in the browser and saved on the account at onboarding, with the optional "How did you hear
about us?" answer; both show in the admin CRM (family timeline, leads list and board, source filter). Page views:
Vercel Web Analytics on public pages. Parent safety page: `/safety`. Engineering follow-ups that serve this plan (no
owner decision needed): the same-day post-trial parent report; a public sample
report page (demo data); a safeguarding page (parent may sit in, lessons only on our Zoom with the waiting room, no
private messages with minors, recording policy, how to report a concern); a review policy page.
Checks still to do by hand before any ad spend: Google Trends exports for the core terms (US, UK, Canada), a reading
pass of Reddit and Muslim parent Facebook groups (not reachable by the research tools), competitors' accounts and the
Meta Ad Library. Paid ads, if ever: only retargeting people who watched our videos, in the Sha'ban run-up (Meta
education leads cost ~$28–35 and religion targeting no longer exists).

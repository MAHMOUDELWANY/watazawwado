# Brand Meaning, Assets and Experience

Started 2026-11-05 in a discussion with the owner. Binding parts are marked **[Decided]**; the rest is proposed and
waits for the owner. Root `RULES.md` and `docs/marketing/RULES.md` win where they disagree.

## 1. Brand meaning [Decided 2026-11-05]

**Brand line (English, primary):** "Not just lessons. A mentor who stays."
Arabic working line: «مش مجرد حصص. مرشد بيفضل معاك.» (to be humanized before public use)

**What it means:** the zaad (provision, from ﴿وتزودوا فإن خير الزاد التقوى﴾) reaches the learner through a person who
knows them and stays with them. Three layers in one idea:
- **Zaad** is the frame: every lesson is something the learner carries for life (the name, the mission).
- **Home** is the relationship: the teacher is a mentor, a guide and a friend, not someone who only passes on information.
- **What lasts** is the effect (﴿وأما ما ينفع الناس فيمكث في الأرض﴾): learning that stays, shown by the same teacher
  over months and by the reports.

**Who it speaks to:** parents and guardians who book for a child, and adults (18+) who book for themselves.

**What we want people to tell their friends** (target word of mouth, never written by us as a review):
- Guardian: "My son actually looks forward to class. He's had the same teacher for months, and I see the change at
  home, not only in the reports."
- Adult learner: "It's the first time a teacher really knows me. Lessons go at my pace and I can see where I am."

**Test for any line or video:** a mother in London gets it in 3 seconds without Arabic; she can repeat it to a
friend in one sentence; the product proves it.

## 2. What we say vs. what only customers say

| We say (actions we can prove) | Only customers say (outcomes) |
|---|---|
| Your teacher stays; we never swap teachers to fill a schedule | "My child changed in how he behaves and thinks" |
| Every teacher is trained to know the learner and shape the lesson around them | "My child loves the teacher" |
| A report after every class | "The prices are fair" |

We never promise outcomes (`docs/marketing/RULES.md` §3.4); outcomes come from customers in their own words.

## 3. "The teacher stays": honest wording [Decided 2026-11-05]

We cannot promise that a teacher never leaves. We can promise:
1. We never change your teacher on our side.
2. A teacher who decides to leave gives two months' notice.
3. If a change happens, the new teacher receives the learner's profile and all reports, so the relationship does not
   start from zero.

## 4. The brand lives in the teacher: "The Watazawwado Way" [Proposed, separate task]

Owner's ideas (2026-11-05), to be designed as their own task:
1. A short course before a teacher starts: our identity, the curriculum rules, the mentor role, student-centred
   lessons, body language, emotional and social intelligence, building rapport; academic methods made simple with
   examples.
2. A learner profile inside the platform, filled by the teacher lesson by lesson (likes games, attention drops after
   ~30 minutes, prefers short explanations, ...), so each lesson is tailored.
3. Conversation questions for every lesson, tied to real life, to build the relationship inside the lesson.
4. All tools inside the platform (games, quizzes, practice); teachers never need another site.
5. A teachers' meeting twice a month for ideas, problems and platform suggestions.
6. Teacher pay above the market average and a possible loyalty bonus after 6/12 months with the same learners (fits
   RULES.md §2c bonus rule); needs numbers against the $9/hour price before any decision.

Why this matters: the owner's schedule will fill up, so the brand cannot rest on one teacher. It rests on the way
every teacher of the academy works (RULES.md §6b).

## 5. Distinctive brand assets [Decided 2026-11-05]

Few, repeated everywhere (site, videos, emails), all from the zaad idea. Arches, gold geometry and generic Islamic
patterns stay secondary because every academy uses them.
1. **Closing line + gesture:** "Pack your zaad." / «جهّز زادك», said at the end of every video with a zipper-closing
   gesture of the hand. It is also the name of the waitlist.
2. **Sound opening (~0.5 s):** a natural sound (a bag zipping shut) with a soft tone; no musical instruments
   (`RULES.md` §1.6). Recitation is never used as a branded sound.
3. **The zaad bag:** a simple bag icon with the khatam star, in crimson, on thumbnails and on the site.
4. **Zad (زاد), the companion** (renamed from Zad/زادي, owner 2026-11-09): a 3D crimson khatam badge with a gold inner star and gold rim, simple eyes and soft cheeks, copper arms ending in gold four-point star hands, copper feet; sixteen expressions with their own effects (gold and teal accents). Shape approved by the owner 2026-11-05, brand colours 2026-11-09 (`src/components/zad/ZadRig.tsx`, showcase `/prototype/zad` in dev); never a human or human-like face, no mouth (owner 2026-11-05) for learners and guardians,
   the counterpart of Jalila for teachers. A rounder, lighter, more playful version for child accounts. Appears only
   at chosen moments (the waitlist, a welcome, the learning guide), small and light, and respects reduced motion.
   In videos it is a recurring character; the audience is guardians and adults, not children.

## 8. Brand experience: nine moments [Decided 2026-11-05]

Principle: people remember the strongest moment and the last one, so these are designed on purpose, with the teacher at
the centre. Zad appears only in moments of joy (waitlist, welcome, celebrations), never in payment, cancellation or
refund messages.

1. **Invitation when a wave opens** ("finally, my turn"): an email with the bag they packed and Zad saying "Your
   place is open". The place is held **72 hours** because the next person is really waiting; one reminder before it
   ends.
2. **First WhatsApp reply** ("real people are here"): ready replies in our voice, each naming the exact next step;
   within 12 hours, within **4 hours** during the weeks a wave opens; signed by the Watazawwado team.
3. **Sign-up and choosing a teacher** ("they remember me"): the track chosen in "Pack your zaad" is preselected and
   Zad says "I kept your bag"; we never ask the same question twice.
4. **The first 10 minutes of the trial** (the moment of truth): two minutes about the person, not the lesson; a small
   success within the first five minutes; at the end "today you learned ___, your plan is ___". Part of the
   Watazawwado Way (§4).
5. **The first report to a guardian** ("they see my child as a person"): one strength, one next step, one sentence
   about the child as a person; sent the same day.
6. **When something goes wrong on our side** (late teacher, lesson we cancel): an apology from the team within hours,
   plain and without excuses, on top of the existing choice (free new time, credit or refund), plus **10 extra minutes
   in the next lesson** as a gift.
7. **Celebrating progress** ("I am really moving"): Zad celebrates the end of a level, a memorized surah and every 10
   lessons, with a share card in the same system as "I packed my zaad" (e.g. "I finished Level 1").
8. **Emails and notifications**: all templates reviewed against the brand line, short and warm; Zad only in welcome
   and celebration messages.
9. **When someone leaves** ("the door is open"): no guilt; one optional one-tap "why?"; "your zaad is saved, come
   back any time"; progress stays saved.

Moments 4–6 go into the Watazawwado Way teacher course.

# CLOSER review of the public pages (2026-11-05)

Pages read on the local site (English): home, About, How it works, Pricing, FAQ. The frame: **C**larify why they are
here, **L**abel the problem, **O**verview what they tried before, **S**ell the outcome, **E**xplain away concerns,
**R**einforce the decision. Checked against `BRAND.md` (brand line, honest launch, nine moments) and `RULES.md`.

## Verdict

The site is strong on **S** (a week with us, progress, the monthly report) and **E** (free trial, no card, female
teacher, safety, cancellation rules). It is weak on **L** and **O**: no page names the family's pain or what they tried
before, so a visitor sees a good product but not "this is for me". The brand line is missing everywhere.

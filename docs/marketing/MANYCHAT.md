# "Comment ZAAD" automation (ManyChat)

Decided 2026-11-05 (`BRAND.md` §6). Anyone who comments **ZAAD** on an Instagram or Facebook post or Reel gets a private
message with the waitlist link. TikTok and YouTube do not allow this the same way, so there the link stays in the bio.
The owner connects the accounts (Claude cannot sign in to them); everything else below is ready to paste.

## Setup (owner, about 15 minutes)

1. Go to manychat.com and sign up **with the Watazawwado Instagram account** (Instagram must be a Professional account
   linked to the Facebook page). Choose the free plan. Check the free plan's current limits on their pricing page before
   relying on it (contact limit and whether comment triggers are included).
2. Connect the Facebook page too.
3. Automation → New automation → trigger **"User comments on your post or reel"** → "Any post or reel" → keyword
   **contains ZAAD** (also add `zaad` and `زاد`).
4. Turn on "Reply to the comment publicly" and paste one of the public replies below (ManyChat rotates them).
5. Add a **Send message** step with the private message below and a button "Pack your zaad" →
   `https://watazawwado.academy/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=zaad-dm`
   (use `utm_source=facebook` in the Facebook copy).
6. Publish, then test from a personal account: comment ZAAD on a post and check the message arrives.

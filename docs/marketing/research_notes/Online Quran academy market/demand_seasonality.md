# Demand and seasonality for online Quran, Tajweed, Arabic and Islamic kids education (Western English-speaking markets), Oct 2026 – Jun 2027

Research date: 2026-10-19. Google Trends could not be queried directly (the Trends API returned HTTP 429 to automated fetches), so no Trends index values appear below. Keyword suggestions were pulled live from Google and YouTube autocomplete (public `suggestqueries.google.com` endpoint, English, default region, 2026-10-19). No search-volume numbers were found for the target phrases; none are invented.

## 1. Expected Islamic calendar dates, Oct 2026 – Jun 2027 (ALL ESTIMATES)

### Takeaway
Ramadan 1448 is projected to start Monday 8 Feb 2027 (Umm al-Qura), Eid al-Fitr Tuesday 9 Mar 2027, Eid al-Adha around Sunday 16 May 2027, and Islamic New Year 1449 around Sunday 6 June 2027. All dates can move by a day with local moon sighting, and Western communities often split between Saudi-following and local-sighting mosques. Rabi al-Awwal does not fall in the window: 1448's started 14 Aug 2026 (already past) and 1449's starts around Aug 2027.

### Cited Findings
- 1448 AH month starts (estimate; page says dates "may shift by 1 day based on moon sighting"): Jumada al-Thani 11 Nov 2026; **Rajab 10 Dec 2026**; **Sha'ban 9 Jan 2027**; **Ramadan 8 Feb 2027**; Shawwal 9 Mar 2027; Dhu al-Qadah 8 Apr 2027; **Dhu al-Hijjah 7 May 2027** — [Truecalendar Hijri 1448](https://truecalendar.com/hijri/1448)
- Isra and Mi'raj (27 Rajab): Tue 5 Jan 2027 (estimate) — [Truecalendar Hijri 1448](https://truecalendar.com/hijri/1448)
- Eid al-Adha (10 Dhu al-Hijjah): Sun 16 May 2027 (estimate); month grid implies Day of Arafah Sat 15 May 2027 — [Truecalendar Hijri 1448](https://truecalendar.com/hijri/1448); [islamic.app](https://islamic.app/hijri/eid-al-adha/2027) also gives 16 May; [Salla](https://salla.com/en/tools/seasons-calendar/seasons/eid-adha-2027) gives 17 May; [Bangladesh Date Today](https://bangladatetoday.com/days-until/eid-ul-adha) gives 18 May for local sighting
- Rabi al-Awwal 1448 began 14 Aug 2026; Mawlid (12 Rabi al-Awwal) 25 Aug 2026 — both before this window — [Truecalendar Hijri 1448](https://truecalendar.com/hijri/1448)
- Ramadan 1448: Umm al-Qura projects start Mon 8 Feb 2027, end Mon 8 Mar, Eid al-Fitr Tue 9 Mar 2027; Saudi announcement expected evening of 7 Feb; astronomical new moon 6 Feb 2027 15:56 UT, crescent expected easily visible worldwide on 8 Feb — [Wego: Ramadan 2027](https://blog.wego.com/ramadan-2027/); [Jordan News (astronomical calculations)](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-20/Middle%20East/Date-of-the-First-Day-of-Ramadan-2027-According-to-Astronomical-Calculations-52316)
- Ramadan may run 29 or 30 days, putting Eid al-Fitr on 9 or 10 Mar 2027 — [Gulf News (UAE astronomers)](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae%2Fwhen-will-ramadan-2027-begin-uae-astronomers-share-latest-moon-sighting-forecasts-1.500705567); some local-sighting countries expect 9 Feb start — [Bangla Date Today](https://www.bangladatetoday.com/blog/ramadan-2027-start-date-bangladesh)
- Likely 2027 dates also published for UAE — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/ramadan-eid-al-fitr-eid-al-adha-2027-likely-dates)
- Islamic New Year 1449 (1 Muharram): about Sun 6 Jun 2027; Umm al-Qura calculation puts 4 Muharram 1449 on 9 Jun 2027, consistent with 6 Jun — [Truecalendar Hijri 1449](https://truecalendar.com/hijri/1449); [Miqatona](https://miqatona.com/date/hijri/1449/01/04)

### Inferences
- Derived from a 1 Ramadan = 8 Feb start (estimate): mid-Sha'ban (15 Sha'ban) ~ 23 Jan 2027; last ten nights begin the evening of ~27 Feb 2027 (night of 21 Ramadan); the 27th night (commonly observed as a likely Laylat al-Qadr) falls on the evening of ~5 Mar 2027. Odd nights: evenings of ~27 Feb, 1, 3, 5, 7 Mar. Shift everything by one day if Ramadan starts 9 Feb.
- First 10 days of Dhul Hijjah: ~7–16 May 2027. Ashura (10 Muharram 1449): ~15 Jun 2027.
- Rajab (from 10 Dec 2026) is the traditional "start preparing for Ramadan" month; it overlaps Western winter break, which gives a strong window for "get ready for Ramadan" Quran/Tajweed offers in late Dec – Jan.
- Ramadan 2027 sits entirely in February–early March: in the UK it overlaps Feb half-term (15–19 Feb 2027), and Eid al-Fitr lands just before many US/Canadian spring/March breaks.

### Gaps
- No official Umm al-Qura document for 1448/1449 was fetched directly; dates come from calendar aggregators that agree with each other. ISNA/Fiqh Council of North America (astronomical calculation) and UK moon-sighting committees' 2027 dates were not checked.
- Laylat al-Qadr is not given a fixed date by any source; dates above are derived.

## 2. Google Trends patterns around Ramadan and the school year

### Takeaway
I could not get Google Trends data for the five target terms (US/UK/CA/AU): Trends blocked automated access and no published article reports Trends curves for "learn quran online", "online quran classes", "quran for kids", "learn arabic" or "tajweed". The only published evidence is general Ramadan search behaviour: searches climb about a week before Ramadan and peak at the start or in the first week. Treat any peak/trough months for the target terms as unverified until someone checks Trends by hand.

### Cited Findings
- Ramadan-related searches rose sharply about one week before fasting began and typically peaked in the first week of Ramadan before dropping steeply (2018 Google Trends analysis) — [The Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/sci-tech/ramadan-related-search-on-google-starts-rising-1526628182)
- Google worldwide data (2021): Ramadan search traffic rose ten-fold in the seven days before the official start, peaking then — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts/ramadan-2021-what-people-are-searching-for-during-the-holy-month-1.1203814)
- UK, 2023 (Google data via trade press): Ramadan recipe searches +125%; Ramadan gifts/hampers +271% year on year; non-Muslim "how long does Ramadan last" type questions +250% — [Food Manufacture](https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2023/04/20/ramadan-web-searches-skyrocket-in-the-uk/)
- In the UAE, overall Google usage rose through Ramadan and peaked toward the end of the month — summarised in [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/changing-search-trends-in-ramadan/) (MENA, not Western)
- Think With Google (MENA): 1,860% year-on-year increase in searches for religious app downloads during Ramadan — cited by [Gould Studio, Designing for Ramadan 2024](https://www.gouldstudio.com/insights/designing-for-ramadan-2024) (secondary source; country context MENA)
- 123 million Google searches related to Ramadan in 2023 (no country breakdown, original source not named) — [Gould Studio](https://www.gouldstudio.com/insights/designing-for-ramadan-2024)
- Language learning shows a New Year spike in general: Duolingo reported its 2020 New Year rise and stronger 2020 growth, with no Arabic-specific or monthly figures; Arabic is not in Duolingo's top 10 languages — [Duolingo 2020 Global Language Report](https://blog.duolingo.com/global-language-report-2020/); [Language Magazine](https://www.languagemagazine.com/2021/01/23/duolingo-releases-global-language-report/)

### Inferences
- Likely (unverified) pattern for the Quran/Tajweed terms: rise from mid-Sha'ban, steepest climb in the 7–10 days before Ramadan (≈ 29 Jan – 7 Feb 2027), peak in Ramadan's first week (≈ 8–15 Feb), with a smaller secondary bump in the last ten nights; fall after Eid (from ~10 Mar). Because the Hijri calendar moves ~11 days earlier each year, a 5-year Trends view should show this peak sliding from April (2022–23) to March (2024–25) to February (2026–27).
- "Learn arabic" probably follows the general language-learning pattern (January resolution spike, back-to-school September) more than Ramadan, but this is not verified.
- "Quran for kids"/kids' classes likely also respond to the school calendar (September enrolment, January restart). Unverified.

### Gaps
- No Trends index values, peak months or trough months for any of the five terms in US/UK/CA/AU. Action: a person should open Google Trends (5-year, each country, "All categories"), export CSVs for the five terms, and add them here.
- No study comparing kids' Islamic education searches to the Western school year was found.

## 3. Western school calendars, 2026–27 (when parents look for activities)

### Takeaway
Winter break is almost uniform: about 21 Dec 2026 – 1 Jan 2027 (US, England, Ontario; Scotland to 5 Jan). Spring breaks scatter across mid-March to mid-April 2027, mostly after Eid al-Fitr. UK has Oct half-term 26–30 Oct 2026 and Feb half-term ~15–19 Feb 2027 (inside Ramadan). Ontario March break is 15–19 Mar 2027.

### Cited Findings
- US: no national calendar; districts set their own — [US School Calendar 2026-27](https://us-school-calendar.com/school-holidays-2026-2027)
- US winter break examples: Michigan Kent County common calendar 21 Dec 2026 – 4 Jan 2027 — [Kent ISD](https://www.kentisd.org/downloads/about_kent_isd/kent_county_common_school_calendar_002.pdf); many Washington districts 21 Dec 2026 – 1 Jan 2027 — [Washington OSPI 2026-27 breaks](https://ospi.k12.wa.us/about-ospi/about-school-districts/2026-27-school-breaks)
- US spring break 2027 examples (state-by-state guide): AZ/TX 15–19 Mar; FL/PA 22–26 Mar; CA/IL/OH 29 Mar – 2 Apr; GA/NC 5–9 Apr — [US School Calendar 2026-27](https://us-school-calendar.com/school-holidays-2026-2027); Ottawa Area ISD (MI): first full week of April (5–9 Apr 2027) — [OAISD common calendar](https://oaisd.org/about-us/common-calendar)
- NYC: one news report gives winter break 24 Dec – 1 Jan and spring break 22–30 Apr 2027, not confirmed against the NYC DOE calendar — via search summary of [Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/en/today/summer-break-will-last-through-august-in-this-school-district-as-classes-resume-on-september-10/) (low confidence)
- England: autumn half-term 26–30 Oct 2026; Christmas 21 Dec 2026 – 1 Jan 2027 — [Save My Exams UK term dates](https://www.savemyexams.com/learning-hub/school-holidays/); Feb half-term 15–19 Feb 2027 — [Royal Greenwich](https://royalgreenwich.gov.uk/info/200285/about_our_schools/448/school_term_dates/5)
- England Easter 2027 example: 26 Mar – 9 Apr 2027 (Lincolnshire school) — [Pilgrim School](https://www.pilgrim.lincs.sch.uk/_site/data/files/documents/term-dates-and-upcoming-events/6200C8C1F6DD5FC485320907C7603C0A.pdf); other councils: [Bath & NE Somerset](https://www.bathnes.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-27%20Final%20Approved%20Term%20Dates.pdf), [Swindon](https://www.swindon.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/11478/swindon_schools_term_and_holiday_dates_2026-27.pdf)

### Inferences
- Parent "activity/enrolment" windows in the period: (a) UK Oct half-term (26–30 Oct 2026); (b) winter break ~21 Dec – 4 Jan, which coincides with Rajab and New Year resolutions; (c) back-to-school restart in early January (new routine); (d) UK Feb half-term inside Ramadan; (e) spring/Easter breaks mid-March to mid-April, right after Eid al-Fitr: a natural "keep the Ramadan momentum" push; (f) summer-holiday planning in May–June, overlapping Dhul Hijjah/Eid al-Adha.
- Typical summer holidays (general knowledge, not sourced here): US roughly late May/June to August/early September; England late July to end of August; Canada end of June to early September. Verify before use.

### Gaps
- Summer 2027 dates for US, UK and Canada were not confirmed. Australia school terms were not researched.
- No source measured when Muslim parents in the West search for Quran/Islamic classes relative to school breaks.

## 5. How Ramadan affects social media use among Muslims

### Takeaway
Use rises during Ramadan, roughly +30% in older MENA studies, and shifts to the evening and late night: peaks before iftar (about 6–7 pm local) and from about midnight to 4 am around suhoor, with TikTok viewing rising after iftar until midnight. Almost all published data is from MENA or global panels; I found no Meta/TikTok/Google study of Western (US/UK) Muslims' Ramadan usage by time of day.

### Cited Findings
- Social media use rose on average 30% during Ramadan in the countries studied (Twitter +33%); late-night use linked to waiting for suhoor (The Online Project, 2013, MENA) — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/news/458121); [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2013/07/social-media-in-ramadan-exploring-arab-user-habits-on-facebook-)
- Peak social hours: before iftar (~6–7 pm) and midnight–4 am; Facebook MENA MD: "time spent on our platforms does indeed increase" — [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/lifestyle/why-social-media-use-spikes-during-ramadan-1.867735) (article date not visible)
- Internet use peaked 12–3 am just before suhoor (Rackspace infographic, 2014) — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2014/07/4-tips-for-brands-targeting-arabs-on-twitter-this-ramadan)
- Peak times vary by country: Qatar spikes right after sunset; Saudi most active ~10 pm (2013) — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2013/07/social-media-in-ramadan-exploring-arab-user-habits-on-facebook-)
- TikTok: users shifted to watching rather than creating; consumption rose after iftar until midnight — [Arab News Pakistan](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2068366/media) (year unclear)
- Muslim creators posted less during Ramadan; most engaging fashion/beauty content was about Eid (UK, 2023 data) — [corq.studio](https://corq.studio/reports/influencer-data-ramadan-and-eid-campaigns-for-2024/)
- 65% spend more time watching video during Ramadan and Eid (Meta "Meet Meta Ramadan Report"); 87% used social media to learn new things during Ramadan (TGM Research 2022 global study); 74% said they spent more time on devices (2022, unsourced) — via [Gould Studio](https://www.gouldstudio.com/insights/designing-for-ramadan-2024) (secondary)
- Turkish survey (n=537): 81% said their social media time increased in Ramadan — [Üsküdar University](https://uskudar.edu.tr/en/new/social-media-usage-increased-during-ramadan/82708)
- Experience of fasting among British Muslims (psychological, social, health behaviours) — [PLOS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0313688) (not mined for screen-time data)

### Inferences
- For Western audiences in Feb–Mar 2027, sunset is early (UK ~5–6 pm, US East ~5:30–6 pm), so the iftar window falls in normal evening time and suhoor is around 5–6 am. Likely best posting windows (unverified for the West): the hour before iftar; ~8–11 pm after iftar/taraweeh; a smaller window before suhoor. Daytime engagement is likely lower.
- "Learning new things" is a stated Ramadan use of social media, which supports short Tajweed/Quran-learning content during the month. Paid conversion pushes are better placed in the pre-Ramadan run-up (Rajab/Sha'ban) and the post-Eid "keep going" window.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 Meta, TikTok or Google report on Ramadan behaviour of Muslims in the US, UK, Canada or Australia was found. Most figures are 2013–2022 MENA data or secondary summaries; treat them as directional.
- No time-of-day data for Western Muslims during Ramadan.

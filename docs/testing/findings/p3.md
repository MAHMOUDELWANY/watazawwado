## P3-001 · Low · PKG-04 · S P · fixed
A discount code whose value is not a number made every total `NaN`: `computeCheckout` returned `NaN` for the discount,
the balance used and the amount to pay. The admin form and the table's CHECK both refuse such a value today, so this
was reachable only through a bad row, but the money function must not depend on that.
- Fix: `server/pricing/discounts.ts` reads a non-numeric value as no discount. Test: `p3-money-units` "a broken code value".

## P3-002 · Medium · BOOK-02, TCH-03 · S P T · every device · production too (from 28 Mar 2027) · fixed
On the day the clocks change in the teacher's time zone, the teacher's hours moved by one hour. Hours 06:00-23:00
became 07:00-24:00 in spring (London 28 Mar, New York 14 Mar, Cairo 30 Apr 2027) and 05:00-22:00 in autumn, so a
family could book 05:00 or 23:30 with a teacher who never offered it. The server's own check made the same mistake, so
it accepted exactly those wrong times.
- Cause: `startOf('day').plus({ minutes })` adds real minutes; on a 23- or 25-hour day minute 360 is not 06:00.
- Fix: `atDayMinute()` in `server/integrations/availabilityEngine.ts`, used by the slot list and the slot check.
  Test: `p3-time-dst` (the old arithmetic gave 07:00 / 05:00 for 06:00 on those four days).
- No change falls inside today's 60-day window, so nothing was booked wrongly.

## P3-003 · Medium · BOOK-03, PKG-02 · V, any account · production too · fixed
`POST /api/payments/create-intent` had no sign-in and no ownership check. Anyone who knew or guessed a booking
reference (`MHM-` + 6 hex digits) or a package id could open its Stripe checkout: the page shows the family's email,
the subject and the price. RULES.md §2: no guests.
- Steps: as another family (or with no session) call the route with someone's pending lesson. Expected 401/404.
  Actual: the request reached Stripe.
- Fix: the route requires a signed-in family and answers 404 unless the lesson or package is theirs
  (api/index.ts); the checkout modal now sends the session (`InstantCheckoutModal.tsx`).
  Tests: `p3-payments-flow` "the checkout cannot be opened for someone else's lesson", `qa-01-money-paths-smoke`.

## P3-004 · High · BOOK-07, BOOK-08 · S P · production too · **open: migration written, the owner applies it**
A family can move or cancel its own lesson straight through the database and skip every rule the server enforces.
`reschedule_booking_by_management` and `cancel_booking_by_management` are leftovers from guest bookings, still
executable by the browser roles. The booking answer returns the lesson's management token, and the public key is in
the page, so:
- a trial or paid lesson is moved to **any** time at least 10 minutes ahead: today, 03:00, outside the teacher's hours
  (rule: tomorrow at the earliest, inside working hours); a trial is moved as often as they like (rule: once); the
  learner's other lesson at that time is not checked;
- a paid lesson is cancelled with **no lesson credit** (the family loses its money), and a trial with no record that
  the server's cancel flow keeps.
- Steps: book a trial; call `rpc/reschedule_booking_by_management` with the anon key, the reference and the token.
  Expected: refused. Actual: the lesson moved to 03:00 Cairo; `cancel_booking_by_management` then cancelled it.
- Nothing in the app calls these two functions (the server moves and cancels in its own routes since P2-009).
- Fix: migration `20261111000000_...` revokes EXECUTE from `anon` and `authenticated`. Until it is applied the hole is
  open. Test: `p3-booking-rules` "P3-004" (marked `todo`, fails today, passes once applied). The Supabase security
  advisor lists the same two functions.

## P3-005 · Medium · BOOK-01, BOOK-07 · S P · production too · fixed
The server accepted a lesson any distance ahead: a hand-made request booked a confirmed free trial three years from
now (the calendar only shows 60 days). The slot would stay taken, the learner's one trial would be used, and reminders
and a meeting would wait for years.
- Fix: `validateSlotAvailability` refuses a start more than 60 days ahead (`MAX_BOOKING_DAYS_AHEAD`), for booking,
  the student's move and the teacher's move alike. Arabic text in `bookingErrors.ts`. Test: `p3-booking-rules` "years ahead".

## P3-006 · Medium · AUTH-07, BOOK-01 · T A · production too · fixed
A teacher's session could book a learner lesson. `/api/bookings/create` reads the token itself instead of going through
`verifyStudentAuth`, so the "teachers cannot use the student portal" rule did not apply: a QA teacher booked a confirmed
free trial for their own email, with no learner (`student_id` null). Such a lesson appears in no family's portal and in
no teacher's student list.
- Fix: a staff account is refused (403); an account with no learner row is told to finish its profile (409
  `ONBOARDING_REQUIRED`). Test: `p3-booking-rules` "who may book".

## P3-007 · High · PKG-03 · S P · production too · fixed in code; the migration closes the last gap
Two bookings sent in the same instant both used a package's last credit: one lesson credit, two confirmed lessons.
The server counted the lessons already booked, then inserted, with nothing in between. The second lesson is taught
for free, and recording it later fails because the package has no credit left.
- Steps: one lesson credit; send two bookings with it at once. Expected: one 201. Actual: 201 and 201.
- Fix now (api/index.ts): after the insert the server counts again in a fixed order and the lesson beyond the credits
  removes itself (400 "all remaining package credits are already booked").
- Fix in the database (same migration as P3-004): trigger `trg_enforce_package_credit` counts under a lock on the
  package row, which also covers a request slower than the other's whole insert and recount.
  Test: `p3-booking-rules` "a lesson credit books one lesson" (2 lessons before the fix).

## P3-008 · Low · BOOK-01 · S P · fixed
A lesson length that is not a number ("thirty") was read as 60 minutes and booked at the 60-minute price.
- Fix: only a missing length falls back to 60; anything else must be 30, 45 or 60. Test: `p3-booking-rules`.

## P3-009 · Medium · BOOK-02 · S P · production too · fixed (review, then a test)
A learner with no teacher (after a teacher is deleted, RULES.md §1; or a parent who books for themselves) could be
booked at any hour: with no teacher there are no working hours to check, and the lesson was stored with no teacher.
- Fix: `/api/bookings/create` answers 409 `NO_TEACHER` when no teacher can be resolved. Test: `p3-booking-rules`
  "a learner with no teacher".

## P3-010 · High · LES-07, AUTH-07 · T · production too · fixed
Any teacher could read the homework of any learner in the academy and decide on it. `canTeacherAccessStudent` returns
`{ exists, allowed }`; `server/routes/homeworkSets.ts` tested the object itself, which is always true.
- `GET /api/dashboard/students/:id/homework-sets`: titles, scores and every question the learner got wrong.
- `POST /api/dashboard/homework-sets/:id/decision`: accept or "retry"; a retry notifies the family.
- Steps: as teacher 2, open the homework of teacher 1's learner. Expected 403. Actual 200.
- Fix: both checks read `.allowed`. Test: `p3-ownership` "a teacher reads and changes only their own learners"
  (found by the sweep over every teacher route, then pinned with a real homework row).

## P3-011 · Low · AUTH-07 · T A · fixed
`GET /api/dashboard/bookings/:id` returned the whole booking row, including `management_token_hash`. The hash cannot be
turned back into the token (192 random bits), but it has no business outside the database.
- Fix: the dashboard's response filter drops `management_token*` for every role. Test: `p3-ownership`.

## P3-012 · Low · INT-01, INT-03 · S P · production too · **open: migration written, the owner applies it**
A signed-in family can select its own rows of `student_intakes` and `learning_offers` straight from the database and
read fields the portal never shows: `assessment`, `pricing_recommendation`, `teacher_notes`, `recommended_price_usd`,
`recommended_tier`. The portal itself reads them only through the API.
- Fix: migration `20261111000001_...` takes SELECT on those tables (and `intake_review_events`) away from the browser
  roles. Test: `p3-db-contract` "P3-012" (`todo` until applied).
- Checked and fine: every table has RLS on; the anon key reads only the active catalogue; a family reads no row of
  another family and a teacher no learner or lesson of another teacher, straight from the database; nobody but the
  server can write a booking, a payment, a credit or a staff account; none of the 25 server functions runs for the
  browser roles.

## P3-013 · Medium · PKG-08, BOOK-06 · S P · production too · fixed
Account balance used on a checkout did not come back when the family let the held time go (or the hold ran out). The
wallet showed 0 until the family opened another checkout, which could be never. USD 3.00 looked lost.
- Fix: letting a hold go, and the automatic release of expired holds, settle the checkout's code and balance at once
  (api/index.ts). Test: `p3-checkout-and-admin-money` "account balance".

## P3-014 · Medium · BOOK-05, BOOK-08 · S P · production too · fixed
A "pay later" lesson that the family then paid for gave nothing back when cancelled in time: no lesson credit, no
refund path. The cancel route decided by the payment plan, not by whether money had been received.
- Fix: a pay-later lesson with a confirmed payment is cancelled into a lesson credit like any paid lesson; unpaid, it
  is simply cancelled. Test: `p3-checkout-and-admin-money` "a pay-later lesson that was then paid".

## P3-015 · Low · ADM-07 · A · fixed
`PATCH /api/dashboard/services/:id` accepted an hourly rate of 0 (and `null`, which is 0): every paid lesson of that
subject would become a held lesson that cannot be paid. An unknown subject answered 500.
- Fix: the rate must be above 0 (and at most 1000); an unknown subject is 404. Test: `p3-checkout-and-admin-money`.

## P3-016 · Low · PUB-03 · V · production too · fixed

## P3-017 · Low · BOOK-03 · S P · fixed
When a Stripe payment was recorded but its confirmation failed half way (a timeout between the two steps), the retry
confirmed the lesson but never queued its meeting, calendar event and reminders, and never settled its discount code.
- Fix: the retry path in `fulfillAutomatedPayment` does what the first pass does after confirming.
  Test: `p3-payments-flow` "a payment recorded but not confirmed".

## P3-018 · Low · BOOK-12 · A · open (review)
"Record payment" without a lesson can never be saved: the table requires every payment to belong to a lesson or a
package (`payments_exactly_one_target_check`), and the admin route sends neither, so it answers 500. Proposed: remove

## P3-019 · Low · BOOK-02 · S P · open (owner decides)
The server accepts any start time inside the teacher's hours, not only the ones the calendar offers: a hand-made
request can book 07:13. It breaks nothing, but it fragments the teacher's day. Proposed: accept only starts on the

## P3-021 · Low · BOOK-03 · S P A · open (owner decides)

a cancelled lesson. Proposed: an admin notice "paid, but the time was released: rebook or refund", and a line for the
family.

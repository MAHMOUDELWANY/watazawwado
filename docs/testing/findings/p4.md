## Persona 1: busy mother in the UK, phone only, English, two children, five minutes

Device 390x844, English. Account `+qa-p4-parent-2` ("QA Sarah P4"), children Maryam (8, girl, Quran reading,
female teacher) and Yusuf (11, boy, Zad Path, male teacher).

### Journey, thinking aloud
1. **Land.** A full-screen language gate comes first ("Choose your language"). English is marked "Suggested" and
   has the focus ring, but it is not selected, and the button stays disabled until she taps it. Two taps to see
   the site she was already reading in English. (`m01`)
2. **Understand.** The hero h1 is "Provision that lasts, for you and your family." The concrete offer (online 1-on-1
   Quran, Tajweed, Zad Path, Arabic, English) is the small teal line above it. She has to work out what is sold.
   The rotating line "One-on-one lessons in English" reads as "taught in English" when the word is the subject
   English. Two teacher photos fill the rest of the first screen. (`m02`, `m03`)
3. After ~2.5 s an "Install Watazawwado" banner drops over the header. She has not signed up; she does not want an
   app yet. It stays over everything that follows, including the sign-up dialog (`z-[60]` over the dialog's
   `z-50`). (`m03`, `m04`, `m05`, `m06`)
4. **Sign up.** "Book your free trial lesson" → dialog "Your first lesson is on us" → "Create an account". The form
   opens on "For myself" (pre-selected); she needs "For my family". The family form asks her age, name, email,
   password, then an emailed code. Google is the fastest path but sits last, below the fold. A large decorative
   arch takes the first 330 px of the dialog. The verse under the logo is translated differently here ("mindfulness
   of God") than on the homepage ("fear of Allah"). (`m05`, `m06`)
5. **Onboarding, step 1 (Who).** Name and age are pre-filled from sign-up (good). Labels read like a form for
   staff: "Country of Residence (Auto-sets timezone & phone code)", "Standard Country Timezone (Automated):",
   "Phone Number (Must have active WhatsApp for coordination)", "Format: 9 digits", a clock with seconds. A
   "Learning journey ticket" card repeats her name. WhatsApp is required with no word on why. (`m08b`)
6. **Onboarding, step 2 (children).** Headline "Choose your provision of knowledge", "Pull your book from the
   shelf", "Traveller 1 in the caravan", "Add another child to the caravan", next to "Children Information &
   Teacher Assignment" and "1 Child(ren)". Three metaphors and one database label on one screen. The child card
   starts on **Boy** and on a **male teacher already selected** (the first male teacher in the list). After she
   taps "Girl", the male teacher stays selected; she has to notice and switch. (`m09`, `m10`)
   - The pre-selected male teacher is a real production teacher, "Ahmed", whose card line is his profile bio:
     **"احمد الاسطورة"** ("Ahmed the legend"). Every real family who opens onboarding sees it. See P4-003.
   - Ustadh Mahmoud, whose photo is in the homepage hero, is not in the list for any subject: he has no active
     weekly hours, so `/api/student/teachers` marks him full and the card is hidden. The homepage shows a teacher
     the family cannot choose. See P4-004.
   - In production no female teacher is licensed for Quran reading, Tajweed or Memorization (Ustadha Afnan holds
     Zad, Seerah and the three languages only). The "Female teacher" tab then disappears without a word
     (`GuideChoice` hides a gender with no teacher). The homepage promises "Choose a male or female teacher". A
     mother who wants a woman for her daughter's Quran is silently given men only. See P4-004.
7. **Review.** "Review & Confirm Profile", "Registered WhatsApp:", "Children & Instructors Breakdown", and each
   child shown as "(beginner)" although Quran reading and the Zad Path have no level to choose (RULES §2b, §2).
8. **First arrival on the portal.** Five things compete before she can book: a time-zone banner, Zad's 10-step
   tour (modal), then a push-notification modal, an "Install the app" card and a placement-test banner. The page
   behind them calls the parent a learner ("QA SARAH P4'S PROGRESS · A new rhythm starts with a lesson this week ·
   Quran reading", known P2-014) and uses "Instructor:" / "Track:". (`m12`, `m13b`)
   - The time-zone banner says lessons are shown in **"Universal Coordinated Time (UTC)"** although she just chose
     the United Kingdom. Right after onboarding the app keeps the zone it read before onboarding (`UTC`, the sign-up
     default) until the next full reload. See P4-002.
9. **Book the trial (4 steps).** Step 1 has no learner selected but already shows "Quran Reading · Beginner" (Maryam's
   subject), so she taps a goal and Continue → error "Choose who the lesson is for." She taps Maryam → the goal she
   had chosen is cleared → error "Please select a suggested goal…". Two errors on step 1. Labels in capitals
   ("TAILORED TO YOUR JOURNEY", "COMMON GOALS FOR THIS SUBJECT (CLICK TO SELECT)", "CHOOSE YOUR BOOKING TYPE",
   "1. SELECT A DATE (UPCOMING 3 WEEKS)") and "click" on a phone. Step 2 lists "60 min · Free" for a trial,
   disabled with no reason (a trial is at most 45 minutes). Step 3 shows 34 start times from **04:00 AM** for an
   8-year-old, with no "after school" grouping. Step 4 says "SELECTED DISCIPLINE", "SESSION INVESTMENT", the date
   as "2026-11-16", and a cancellation rule that is wrong for a trial (see P4-006). (`m14`–`m17`)
10. **Confirmed.** The confirmation opens scrolled to the bottom: the "Your Free Trial is Booked" headline is off
    screen and the first thing she sees is the middle of a card. It promises reminders "24 hours and 1 hour before"
    (RULES §2: email at booking and 3 hours before, on-site 12 h and 2 h). It never names Maryam's teacher. The big
    button is "WhatsApp us"; nothing offers "Now book Yusuf's trial". The reference starts "MHM-". (`m18`, `m18b`)
11. **Come back.** Home says Maryam's next lesson is **Mon 16 Nov, 6:00 PM**; the Lessons page says **5:00 PM
    Europe/London** for the same booking. Home formats in the device's zone, every other page in the account's zone.
    See P4-001. (`m19`, `m20`)

## Persona 5: sharp, skeptical father, desktop, English, compares prices, reads the legal pages

Desktop 1440x900, device zone America/New_York. Account `+qa-p4-parent-3` ("QA Omar P4"), one son Adam (10, Quran
reading, male teacher).

### Journey, thinking aloud
1. **Pricing first.** The language gate again (deep links too). The h1 "Clear prices" types itself in, so for a
   second the heading reads "Clear pric". Above the fold there is **no price at all**: "Choose a subject to see its
   packages" with eight chips and none selected; "$9 an hour / from $13" is one grey paragraph under the chips. He
   has to click a subject to see a number. Once he does, the packages are honest and easy to check (8 × $8.25 =
   $66, "you save $6"). Packages are shown for 60 minutes only; a parent of a 10-year-old wants 30. (`f01`–`f03`)
2. **Legal.** The terms page is the best page on the site for him: a three-line summary on top, version and date,

   address has no building number or postcode ("ul. Stefana Czarnieckiego, Wrocław"). The pricing page says "Card,
   Apple Pay or PayPal, through Stripe" (PayPal is its own checkout in the code). (`f04`)
3. **Sign up, onboarding.** As persona 1 (same findings). Country detected as United States from his device. Right
   after onboarding, the banner says lessons are shown in "Universal Coordinated Time (UTC)" and the booking
   calendar says **"Times shown in your local time: UTC"** with **04:00 AM already selected**. On his clock that is
   11 pm. If he books "5:00 PM" he gets noon. P4-002 confirmed. (`f05`, `f06`)
4. **Desktop layout.** Two navigations at once: a dark icon rail on the left (no labels) and a top pill bar
   (Home, Lessons, Book, My curriculum, Homework) plus a third cluster on the right (help, bell, language, theme,
   avatar). He does not know which one is "the menu". (`f06`)
5. **Buy a package.** My wallet greets him with "Your booking is saved. If you buy a package, go back…" (left over
   from the half-done trial), three zero counters, then "Remaining Credits 0 / Completed Lessons 0 / Total Granted 0"
   again, and "Authoritative audit trail of package activations". "Book with a new package" takes him **back into
   the half-done free-trial booking at step 3**, still on UTC although he had just pressed "Use my device time" (the
   saved draft keeps its own zone). "Start over", step 2, "Regular 1-on-1 Lesson": a clear table (one lesson $4.50,
   4 × $4.25, 8 × $4.13, 12 × $4.00, 30 min). (`f07`–`f09`)
6. **Checkout.** He chooses 4 lessons and "Pay for the package". The page scrolls to the consent box, which sits
   under the whole grid, so the card next to "You pay $17 for the package" is the **12-lesson card ("Save $6")**.
   His chosen card is off screen above. He taps the sentence "I have read and accept the terms…" to tick it: the
   link inside opens the terms in a new tab and the box stays empty → "Please tick both boxes". Second try: the
   Stripe dialog shows the right sum, $17, as "Package & family savings −$1.00" for a one-child family. Stopped here

### P4-021 · Medium · Arabic pages show English day names, months and AM/PM
- Booking step 3 day strip ("FRI 13 Nov"), slot buttons ("04:00 AM"), review date ("2026-11-16"), confirmation time.
- Fix: format with the page locale (`ar` → "الجمعة ١٣ نوفمبر", "١٠:٠٠ ص" or 24-hour "10:00"); one shared date helper.

### P4-022 · Medium · Small checkboxes and controls for a mis-tapping user
- Terms box 17x20 px (`main input[type=checkbox]` on booking step 4), the sign-up "18 or older" box 18 px. The
  design system asks for 44x44 px. Make the whole row the target (label wraps the input, min-height 44 px).

### P4-023 · Medium · Arabic voice drifts between Egyptian and office Arabic
- RULES §6: 80% simple Fusha, 20% light Egyptian. Zad's tour and onboarding helper lines are close to 100% Egyptian
  ("هوريك… زرار زرار", "دول المعلمين اللي بيدرّسوا… ووقتهم فاضي", "بيتجهز", "سيبه زي ما هو"); review and booking
  screens are formal office Arabic ("الاعتماد النهائي لحسابك التعليمي", "المعلم المعين", "رسوم الجلسة"). One pass
  with `humanizer` over onboarding, booking and the tour in the 80/20 voice.

### P4-024 · Low · Large text: the time-zone banner's button text is cut off; book spines unreadable
- `src/components/ui/TimezoneCheck.tsx` buttons have a fixed height; let them wrap. Put the subject name under each
  book, horizontally, instead of on the spine.

### P4-025 · High (flow broken for children) · "I have a code from my parent" is hidden behind the sign-in dialog
- Flow: AUTH-09 · child · phone, EN. `/student/login` auto-opens the sign-in dialog; the kid link sits under it.
- Fix: put "I have a code from my parent" inside the dialog, above Email, as a big secondary button, and give the
  parent a direct link (`/kid?code=…`, already offered as "copy a link") as the first option.

### P4-026 · Medium · The six code boxes drop digits when typed quickly
- `/kid` code entry: fast input lands in the same box. Accept the whole string in one field (`autocomplete=
  "one-time-code"`, one input with letter-spacing) or handle `input` events that carry several characters.

### P4-027 · Medium (redesign) · The kid space speaks to adults
- Rewrite the kid cards for a child: "Your next lesson: Monday 5 pm with Ustadha Afnan", "Homework: 2 things to do",
  "Play the last lesson's game". Hide anything that leads to "This one is for your parent" (booking CTAs on the kid
  curriculum page). Tab labels at least 12.5 px (design system micro size).

### P4-028 · Idea · A "child mode" on the parent's phone
- Many children use the parent's phone. "Hand the phone to Maryam" from the family home: the kid space for that
  child, exit with the parent's 4-digit PIN or Face ID. Reuses the `/kid` screens.

### P4-029 · Medium (safeguarding) · "Switch to a family account" lets a minor become the guardian
- Flow: AUTH-02/AUTH-03 · teen · phone. The age rules are right; the switch keeps the minor's own sign-up as the
  guardian account and only asks for an age.
- Fix: for an under-18 on a personal account, replace "Switch to a family account" with "Ask a parent to join":
  a ready WhatsApp/email message with a link that starts a family sign-up and pre-fills the child (name, age,
  subject). Keep the minor's own account unusable until a parent's account adds him.

### P4-030 · Low · Teens get the children's look
- Version by age like the Zad Path does (Kids 6–11 / Teens 12–17): for 12–17 drop the "Child" badge ("Learner"),
  use a calmer kid space without the mascot greeting, and shorter, direct copy.

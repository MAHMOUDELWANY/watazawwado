# Seerah · the whole series · MAP (Kids · Teens & Adults)

From before the birth to the death of the Prophet ﷺ, in the order of *The Sealed Nectar*, completed and checked from
Ibn Hisham and the hadith collections (every fact verified; disputed reports marked or left out). One episode = one
60-minute lesson of about 20 slides. Kids, Teens & Adults: the same episodes in the same order; Kids slightly shorter.
Every episode gives a companion (or a family member) a real moment, not only the Prophet ﷺ. Owner's rules:
`DECISIONS-2026-10-26.md` §10. Status: Claude's draft, 2026-10-26, for the owner's review.

**46 episodes in 6 seasons.** The column "What the learner carries" is the identity and pride we aim for; it is never
written as a label on the slide.

## Every episode (Kids · Teens & Adults)
- About 20 slides, 60 minutes; Kids slightly shorter in text, same story.
- Told as a series: a hook, stakes, a turning point, a moment that moves the heart, a cliffhanger to the next episode.
- The story is carried by images (the owner's, from Claude's prompts: Kids between childlike and cinematic; Teens &
  Adults cinematic) and motion graphics (maps, routes, numbers, timelines). No faces, ever.
- Ayat and hadith wherever they serve the story, each verified.
- A light review that shows whether the learner was with us (not an exam), and one action for the week.

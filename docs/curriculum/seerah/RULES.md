# Seerah · binding rules (Kids; Teens & Adults)

Owner's decisions 2026-10-25 (design §4.9). `docs/curriculum/RULES.md` wins on conflict.

## Source and accuracy
- Facts and their order: *The Sealed Nectar* (Ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum, English edition, in `Documents\watazawwado-sources\seerah`).
  Our wording only. Reports the book itself calls disputed, or that are weak on Dorar, are marked "it is reported" in
  the teacher notes or dropped. Details the book does not carry (e.g. the camels conversation, from Ibn Ishaq) are named
  by their source and flagged for the owner.
- Dates and numbers as the book gives them ("sixty thousand", "nine or thirteen elephants", 571 CE), said as "the
  sources report".
- The Prophet ﷺ, the companions and angels are never drawn, painted or voiced by a clone. People in images: from
  behind, in silhouette, or far away.

## Story: a series, not a sermon
- One episode per lesson, about 20 pages, told like a drama series: stakes, motive, numbers that build tension, a
  reversal, quiet dignity against force, and a cliffhanger that makes the next episode necessary.
- Lessons are taught without announcing them: each moral is a short **Identity lesson · I'tizaz** line tied to what a
  person in the story just did, and from there to the learner's life (school, friends, being outnumbered).
- Reflection interludes ask one real dilemma by human logic, then turn it to the learner. Integrated lessons state one
  principle as a simple formula, shown through the story (tawakkul = full effort + full surrender).

## The episode template (from the owner's deck, Episode 1)
| Page kind | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Kicker (CHAPTER VII — THE INVINCIBLE VANGUARD), two-line title, one Arabic key term in gold, 2–3 fact or stat cards, "The Chronicle" (3–4 short paragraphs as build steps), the I'tizaz line |
| Interlude · Reflection | Symbolic image, "The question", a paragraph that turns it to the learner |
| Integrated lesson | The principle as a formula, two halves, the I'tizaz line |
| Quote card | A saying from the story with who said it, where |
| Quran verdict | Arabic, English meaning, reference, tadabbur stop |
| Stamp | Date and place (571 CE · عام الفيل) |
| Cliffhanger | "To be continued", two mysteries, "Coming next: Episode NN — …" |

## Look
- Teens & Adults: the deck's look on our motion stage: dark, cinematic, gold serif titles, the owner's stills
  (prompts per page from Claude, images by the owner), motion that explains (maps, numbers, routes).
- Kids: their own tone, simpler sentences, the Kids look and animation; same facts. Every story page has a painting
  that fills the whole slide (owner, 2026-10-27: no window), with a paper wash on the text side and the Zad Kids cast in
  front.
- Banners: every subject and version door has its own banner (`docs/curriculum/BANNER-PROMPTS.md`), never an episode
  image.

## Images (owner, 2026-10-27)
- Claude writes every image prompt in `IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`, several episodes in one batch, each prompt self-contained
  (the owner pastes them into ChatGPT); the images land in `watazawwado-archive\seerah\<version>-epNN\`.
- No two images in the series repeat a look: before a new batch, read the visual registry at the end of
  `IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`, follow its rules, and add the new images to it.

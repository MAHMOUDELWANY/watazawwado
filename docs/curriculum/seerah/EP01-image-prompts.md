# Seerah · Teens & Adults · Episode 1: The Year of the Elephant — image prompts

For the owner to generate (design §4.9). One image per slide, in the look of the owner's deck: dark, cinematic, golden
light, rich detail. Save each as `public/curriculum/seerah-teens/ep01/<file>.webp` (or send them and Claude places them).

## Base prompt (put before every prompt)

> Cinematic historical matte painting, photorealistic, 6th-century Arabia, dramatic golden-hour or torch light, deep
> shadows, amber and bronze palette with touches of crimson and teal, volumetric light and dust, epic scale, shallow depth
> of field, 16:9 landscape, no text, no letters, no logos. No visible faces: people only from behind, in silhouette, or far
> away. Never show a prophet, an angel or a companion.

Leave the right third of the frame calmer (darker or simpler): the text panel sits there.

## Notes for the build
- The Quran card (Al-Fil 105:1–5) and the quote card are drawn in code, no image.
- Two of the deck's slides change, from the source check: the three "signs at the birth" (the Persian fire, the palace
  towers) are reported weakly; *The Sealed Nectar* itself says they are disputed (al-Bayhaqi; not accepted by al-Ghazali),
  so that slide becomes "An orphan from the start" (slide 21). The year follows the book: 571 CE, not 570.
- The camels conversation (slides 11–12) is in Ibn Ishaq (Ibn Hisham), not in *The Sealed Nectar*; it stays, attributed
  to Ibn Ishaq, for the owner to confirm.

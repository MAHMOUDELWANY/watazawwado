## Goal

The learner can show, with reasons, that the universe has a Maker, and answer "the Big Bang explains everything, so who
needs God?" calmly, without being thrown by it.

## Outcomes

1. States the rule "everything that **begins** has a cause" and gives everyday examples.
2. Knows that science dates the universe back to a beginning (about 13.8 billion years, NASA) and draws the conclusion.
3. Gives the three options of At-Tur 52:35 (from nothing, made itself, made by a Maker) and why only the third holds.
4. Explains that science answers *how* things work, not *whether* there is a Maker (the cake and the baker).
5. Answers "Then who made God?": the rule is about what begins; Allah is al-Awwal, with nothing before Him (57:3, Muslim 2713).
6. Spots the flaw in at least 7 of 8 statements in the challenge.

## Spark

Recall of Lesson 1 (the three doors, "he came to teach you your deen") and "How did your challenge go?". Then the
cliffhanger from Lesson 1 plays out: in science class someone says "So the universe explains itself. Who needs God?"
The learner answers first.

## Rational steps (before the text)

1. **What begins has a cause.** Your phone, this lesson, you: none of them started themselves. Nobody seriously thinks a
   phone appears with no cause.
2. **The universe began.** Scientists trace it back about 13.8 billion years (NASA, "Universe overview"). The Big Bang
   does not remove the question; it makes it sharper: what started it?
3. **Only three options.** It came from nothing, it made itself, or something outside it made it. Nothing makes nothing;
   making yourself means existing before you exist. One option is left.
4. **How is not who.** Chemistry explains how a cake rises; it does not mean nobody baked it.
5. **Who made God?** The rule is about things that *begin*. A Maker of time and matter is not inside time; Allah has no
   beginning (al-Awwal). Asking who made Him is like asking what is north of the North Pole.

## Not in this lesson (Claude's calls)

- No fine-tuning or "design" arguments that rest on contested science; only the beginning of the universe, which is the
  mainstream view.
- "Who made God?" is answered because the argument itself invites it for this age; it is answered in one page, calmly,
  and closed.

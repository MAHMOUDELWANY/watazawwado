# ChatGPT prompts: Zad Essentials · Teens (characters + Lesson 1)

For the owner. Open **one new ChatGPT chat**, paste the setup message first, then send the numbered prompts **one at a
time, in order**, waiting for each image. Send the images back to Claude with their numbers. Style rules:
`../style/teens.md`.

---

## Setup message (send this first)

```
I'm making images for an online Islamic course for Muslim teenagers (ages 12 to 17) who live in the West. I will send you prompts one by one; please make exactly one image per prompt and keep everything consistent across the whole chat.

STYLE (every image): modern anime film illustration, clean confident line art, cel shading with soft gradients, cinematic lighting, detailed believable backgrounds. Natural teen proportions, expressive eyes, no chibi, no exaggerated features. Colour accents: deep teal, crimson, copper and warm gold.

RULES (every image):
- Everyone is dressed modestly. Girls wear loose clothes and a hijab that covers hair and neck.
- Never show the face or body of any prophet, any companion of the Prophet, or the angel Jibril. When a prompt mentions "the stranger", show only a figure in white clothes seen from behind, small and far away.
- No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the image. Phone screens show only soft glowing shapes.
- Scenes: 4:3 landscape. Characters: one character, full body, transparent background (PNG); if transparency is not possible, a plain pure white background.

THE CHARACTERS (keep them exactly the same in every image):
1. Zayn, 15-year-old boy: short black hair faded at the sides, light brown skin, dark brown eyes, slim; olive-green bomber jacket over a white T-shirt, black joggers, white trainers.
2. Noor, Zayn's 13-year-old sister: same skin and eyes as Zayn; crimson hijab; oversized cream hoodie; long loose dark-teal skirt to the ankles; white trainers; phone in a gold case.
3. Ethan, Zayn's 15-year-old friend: messy ginger-blond hair, light freckles, fair skin; navy hoodie with one grey stripe on the sleeves; blue jeans; headphones around his neck.
4. Amira, Noor's 13-year-old friend: olive skin; light grey hijab; oversized navy jumper; long loose black trousers; often one earbud in; quiet, guarded face.
5. Hamza, their 22-year-old cousin: neat short black beard, black hair, round thin-framed glasses; navy quarter-zip jumper; university lanyard; calm warm smile.
6. Aunt Salma, a woman in her 40s: soft beige hijab; plum abaya; kind eyes; a small gold ring.

Please reply "Ready" and wait for my first prompt.
```

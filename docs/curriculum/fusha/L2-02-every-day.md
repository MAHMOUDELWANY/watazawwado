**One question:** Lesson 1 put the person at the back of the verb. Where does it go when something happens every day?
Answer: at the front (أَ، نَ، يَ، تَ), and for three persons at both ends.

Device (`g-fv2.tsx`, Lesson 1's colours): a whole-word tile with a "front tag" on its right edge (the side Arabic starts
on); a "back tag" on the left where a form also has an ending. Wow: Kids, the same "who" moves from the back of
ذَهَبْتُ to the front of أَذْهَبُ; 12+, the past and present tables read across, and the memory word أَنَيْتُ. 12+ also
learns verbs in pairs (كَتَبَ يَكْتُبُ) because the middle vowel cannot be predicted.

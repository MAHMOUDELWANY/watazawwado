# Fusha · Level 2 (Intermediate) · Kids and 12+ · MAP

Spine: the order of structures in *Madinah Arabic Reader* 3–5, with themes, conversation and reading from
*Lughatuna al-Fusha* 2; every sentence, dialogue and scene is ours (`fusha/RULES.md` §2). Status: Claude's draft,
2026-11-03, for the owner's review. Plan: `LEVELS-PLAN-2026-11.md` §2.

**What Level 2 adds.** Level 1 builds the noun sentence and meets the past verb. Level 2 makes the learner act and tell:
the verb in every person and tense, the commands of daily life, numbers and time, and longer stories. Each lesson still
opens with a scene the learner follows before any rule (RULES §3).

**Two versions, one spine.** Kids keep the Level 1 cast (Maryam and Yusuf, Jiddo, Mishmish) at home, school and the
park; 12+ keeps the institute (Yusuf, Sara, Ustadh Kareem, Ustadha Huda) and the city. Kids take one structure per
lesson; 12+ may join two where they belong together.

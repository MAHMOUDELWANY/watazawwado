# Fusha · Level 1 · 12+ (Teens and Adults) · MAP

Same spine as Kids (Madinah Arabic Reader 1–2), faster: two structures per lesson where they belong together, and
themes for a teen or adult abroad at an Arabic institute (Yusuf, a new student; Sara, a classmate; Ustadh Kareem; Ustadha
Huda). Status: Claude's draft, 2026-10-26, for the owner's review.

# Fusha · Level 3 (Advanced) · Kids and 12+ · MAP

Spine: the order of structures in *Madinah Arabic Reader* 6 and 8, with themes, conversation and reading from
*Lughatuna al-Fusha* 3; every sentence, dialogue and scene is ours (`fusha/RULES.md` §2). Status: Claude's draft,
2026-11-03, for the owner's review. Plan: `LEVELS-PLAN-2026-11.md` §2.

**What Level 3 adds.** Level 2 made the learner act and tell in every person and tense. Level 3 opens the machinery
behind the words: one root becomes a family of verbs by its pattern (وَزْن), the doer can disappear (the passive),
and little words change the endings of a whole sentence (إِنَّ and كَانَ). By the end the learner reads a short
unvowelled text and understands how it is built.

**Two versions, one spine.** Kids keep the Level 1–2 cast (Maryam and Yusuf, Jiddo, Mishmish) and take the lighter
half of each structure (Form II and III before the rest; إِنَّ and لَكِنَّ before the other sisters). 12+ keeps the
institute (Yusuf, Sara, Ustadh Kareem, Ustadha Huda) and takes the full structure, with a short reading every lesson.

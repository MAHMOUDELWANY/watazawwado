**One question:** why does the same word for "father" end in three different ways (أَبُو، أَبَا، أَبِي)?

Device (`g-fv3.tsx`): whole-word tiles coloured by their long vowel (ـُو teal, ـَا gold, ـِي crimson); a little word
slides in before the noun and the tile swaps whole to its i-form. Wow: Kids, the child's own name inside a parent's
kunya; 12+, the five nouns as the loud version of the case endings (أَبُو / ٱلْوَلَدُ ...). Abu Hurayrah's kunya is
given as "reports say", for the owner to confirm the wording.

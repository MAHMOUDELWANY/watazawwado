Device (`g-fx1.tsx`): the forge. Root letters as separate tiles drop into a pattern mould and the mould stamps the whole
verb (Form I ink, II teal/shadda, III gold/long ā, IV crimson/hamza). Wow: Kids, Arabic as a kitchen of roots and
cutters; 12+, one vowel (yu) tells you a verb has a pattern.

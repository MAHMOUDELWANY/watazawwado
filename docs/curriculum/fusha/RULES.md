# Fusha Arabic · binding rules (all versions)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-subjects-curricula-design.md` §4.5 and §5. Wins over nothing above it:
`docs/curriculum/RULES.md` and the design win on conflict.

1. **Learners already read** (Literacy A first). Fusha teaches understanding and speaking, three levels.
2. **Scope only from books:** *Madinah Arabic Reader* 1–8 for the grammar spine (one structure per lesson), *Lughatuna
   al-Fusha* 2–3 for themes, conversation and reading. Never their sentences, dialogues, drills or pictures. The aim is
   the best course we can make from all of them (owner, 2026-10-25).
3. **Comprehensible input first:** every lesson opens with a scene or story the learner follows from pictures, motion
   and known words, before any rule. Then the structure, then practice, then a short conversation.
4. **English notes around Arabic input:** the Arabic stays Arabic; the explanation is short, clear English beside it;
   teacher notes in Arabic.
5. **Every Arabic word is fully diacritized** and playable (Mahmoud Fusha clone). Every new word goes in the page's
   `vocab`: it becomes the learner's spaced-repetition deck.
6. **Teacher-proof:** warm-up review, input, practice, conversation prompts and the mission are all in the lesson. The
   teacher never needs outside material.
7. **Neutral everyday content:** families, school, home, food, the city. Islamic greetings and names are fine (they are
   the language), religious teaching is not the subject here.
8. Versions: Kids (6–11) with the literacy cast and look; 12+ later.

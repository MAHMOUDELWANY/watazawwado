# Lesson 1 brief: Dots Make the Difference (Kids)

**Driving question:** Lina and Sam find a note with three shapes that look the same. Are they the same letter?
**Stations:** spark (the note; guess) → zad (alif; ب one dot under: b; ت two dots on top: t; ث three dots on top: th,
"three dots, th, like three"; the wow: one body, the dots move and the letter changes) → athar (tap the right letter;
the game: how many dots?; read and build the word بَاب, door) → ahd (write the boat family at home and teach the "three
dots" trick) → next (the cup family: a dot inside, a dot on top, or none).
**Sounds:** every letter and word on tap: single letter sounds recorded by the owner; the word بَاب by the Mahmoud Fusha clone.
**No religious content.** Cast: the literacy cast (look chosen by the owner); names Lina and Sam.

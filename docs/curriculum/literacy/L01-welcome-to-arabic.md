# Lesson 1 brief: Welcome to Arabic (Kids · Teens · Adults)

Replaces the three Lesson 1 prototypes of 2026-10-26 (`PLAN.md` "Lesson 1 in every version"). Same scope in every
version; each age has its own journey, cast and voice. Claude's draft, 2026-10-26, for the owner's review.

**Driving question:** a line of Arabic the learner cannot read yet (Kids: the sign over the first station; Teens: a
message on Karim's phone; Adults: a shop sign). *Where do I even start?* By the end of the lesson the learner knows
where to start (on the right), what the pieces are (letters that hold hands, dots, little marks) and finds every alif in
it.

**Neutral and modest** (DECISIONS §7, §13): no religious teaching, neutral praise ("Well done!", "You found it!"),
everyday words.

| 4 | zad | **Right to left** | An English word is read by a finger left to right; then the Arabic sign, right to left, and the train turns to face left: in Arabic we start on the right |
| 5 | zad | **28 letters, no capitals** | 28 letter cards fan out of Hibr's bag; an English "A / a" pair splits, the Arabic letter has no twin: one letter, no big and small |
| 6 | zad | **Letters hold hands** | Two letters slide together and join into a word; one letter lets go of the next: most letters join, a few never do |
| 7 | zad | **Dots make letters** (wow) | One boat body; one dot below, two on top, three on top: ب ت ث appear as the dots move: same body, new letter |
| 8 | zad | **Little marks are vowels** | A small slanted line drops onto ب and it says "ba": the short vowels are little marks above and below |
| 9 | zad | **Sounds English doesn't have** | ح ع ق خ float up; Hibr tries ح and fogs his glasses; the child tries with a hand on the throat: new sounds, made deeper |
| 10 | zad | **Meet alif** | Alif is written top to bottom, stroke by stroke, and says "aa"; the learner writes it on paper with the teacher |
| 11 | athar | **True or false?** | Sort game: Arabic starts on the right · it has capitals · 28 letters · letters never hold hands · dots change a letter · alif from the bottom up |
| 12 | athar | **Read the sign** | Back to the sign over the train (قِطَارُ الحُرُوف): start on the right and tap every alif (ل is the look-alike) |
| 13 | ahd | **Our first stamp** | The ticket gets stamp 1; a carriage joins the train carrying ا; mission: find Arabic writing around you and spot an alif |
| 14 | ahd | **Next time…** | Three boats on the platform, each with its own dots: ب ت ث, the boat family (Lesson 2) |

**Teens (Decode the City):** Karim's phone gets a message he can't read; the same beats as "codes" (direction, 28
symbols, joining, dots, marks, new sounds); the first block of the night city lights up at the end. Lina leads the
decoding. **Adults (The Reading Map):** a shop sign; no cast; the route shows the first milestone, "I know how Arabic
works"; facts told plainly, a little more about the sounds and the alphabet's shape families.

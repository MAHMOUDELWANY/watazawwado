# Literacy A · Reading Arabic · Adults (18+) · MAP

Same scope and order as the Kids map (`MAP.md`: letters → joining → short vowels → sukoon, shadda, tanween → long vowels
→ reading), at an adult pace: two letter families per lesson after the first, the "why" behind the script, and reading
real-world text (signs, labels, menus) as the goal. No religious content (design §4.1): neutral words and a neutral
cast. Learners who want the Quran continue to Literacy B; Fusha and Egyptian learners go straight to their course.

Status: Claude's draft, 2026-10-26, for the owner's review.

# Literacy B · Quranic reading rules · MAP (Kids look first)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-subjects-curricula-design.md` §4.2. For Muslims who finished Literacy A: what
the Mushaf adds to ordinary Arabic script. One content for every age; the look follows the learner's age (Kids first).
Scope from Jannat al-Quran *Arabic & Tajweed* Book 1 (later sections), *Nour al-Bayan*, *Fath ar-Rahman* and the King
Fahd Complex mushaf conventions; order, wording and examples are ours. Quran text: Tanzil Uthmani (via the Quran
Foundation API, unchanged). Ayah audio: Husary (muallim), streamed.

Status: Claude's draft map, 2026-10-25, for the owner's review.

| # | Lesson | What the learner can do after it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Same Letters, New Marks | Finds the parts of a Mushaf page and spots the helper marks that storybooks do not have |
| 2 | The Alif That Stands Up | Reads the standing alif (ـٰ) and the small waw and yaa (ۥ ۦ) as long vowels |
| 3 | Written, Not Read | Skips the letters the Mushaf writes but we do not say (ـُوا, أَنَا, the small zero ۟) |
| 4 | The Quiet Alif | Starts and joins on hamzat al-wasl (ٱ) correctly |
| 5 | The Sun and the Moon | Reads al- before sun and moon letters (ٱلشَّمْس, ٱلْقَمَر) |
| 6 | The Name of Allah | Says the lam in Allah heavy or light from the vowel before it |
| 7 | The Wavy Line | Stretches at the madd sign (~) and knows it means "longer" |
| 8 | Tanween, the Mushaf Way | Reads the three ways the Mushaf writes tanween, and the small meem (ۢ) |
| 9 | Where to Stop | Reads the waqf signs (مـ ج صلى قلى لا ∴) and the ayah circle |
| 10 | Stopping on a Word | Changes the last sound when stopping (sukoon, -an → -aa, ة → ه) |
| 11 | Letters With Names | Reads the opening letters (الٓمٓ، يسٓ) by their names |
| 12 | Reading a Whole Surah | Reads a short surah from the Mushaf with every mark, unaided |

# Lesson 1 brief: Same Letters, New Marks (Kids look)

**Driving question:** Yusuf reads his storybook without a stop. Jiddo hands him the Mushaf and he stops at the very first
line. Same letters... so what are those little marks?

**Stations**
- Spark: the cover; Yusuf's storybook vs the Mushaf (ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ glows with marks he has never seen); "what are they?"
- Zad: the hadith "the best of you learn the Quran and teach it" (Sahih al-Bukhari 5027, narrated by Uthman ibn Affan);
  the map of a Mushaf page (surah name, basmala, ayah circles and their numbers, juz marker); the ayah circle = the end
  of an ayah, Al-Fatiha has seven; meet three helpers (ٱ, ـٰ, ۥ); **wow:** the Mushaf in your hand is written the way
  the copies Uthman sent out were written (Madinah, Makkah, Sham, Kufa, Basra), so the marks are a map to reading it
  exactly as it was taught.
- Athar: tap the words with a helper mark (Al-Fatiha); the game: storybook mark or Mushaf helper?; read along with the
  reciter (1:2).
- Ahd: the Mushaf mission at home (three ayah circles, one standing alif, with someone in the family).
- Next: "The alif that stands up: why is a tiny alif standing on a letter, and why do we stretch it?"

**Evidence log**
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5027: «خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ», narrated by Uthman ibn Affan; sahih (in al-Bukhari).
  English meaning ours. Checked on Dorar 2026-10-26: sahih (al-Bukhari 5027; also Abu Dawud 1452, at-Tirmidhi 2907).
- Ayat 1:1–1:7, 112:4: Tanzil Uthmani text through the Quran Foundation API, 2026-10-25, unchanged.
- Uthman's copies sent to the cities: the standard account (al-Bukhari 4987 on the compilation; the list of cities from the
  books of Quran sciences). Shown without numbers of copies, which the sources differ on.

**Not in this lesson (Claude's calls):** no tajweed rules; the names of the marks stay simple (helper marks), their rules
come one per lesson from Lesson 2.

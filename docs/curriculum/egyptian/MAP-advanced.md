# Egyptian Arabic · Advanced · MAP (Adults, Teens, Kids)

Scope and order: the functions of *Mazboot*, *fi Kull Haaga* and *Advanced Learner* (Egyptian Colloquial), re-told in
our own scenes (RULES.md). Status: Claude's draft, 2026-11-04, for the owner's review.

The learner finished Intermediate: past, future, stories. Advanced is about sounding like a person, not a textbook:
opinions, the fixed phrases Egyptians live by, and real speed.

## Teens (12–17)

The club's last summer: arguing about the team captain, memes and idioms, a voice note from a friend at full speed.
1 "Who Should Be Captain?" (opinions) · 2 "That's What They Say" (idioms in memes and chats) · 3 "Say It Again,
Slower!" (a fast voice note).

## Kids (6–11) — a lighter Advanced

Stories and jokes at Teta's: 1 "I Think…" (أنا شايف / شايفة، with choices: which ice cream?) · 2 "Teta's Sayings"
(two or three kind proverbs) · 3 "Ziad's Joke" (a short Egyptian joke and why it is funny).

Notes
- Proverbs and idioms are taught as the people's sayings, in their real setting; none is attributed to the Prophet ﷺ
  or presented as religious text.
- Egyptian lines are recorded by the owner (no generated Egyptian voice).

# Egyptian Arabic · Adults · Lesson 1 · Ahlan! What's Your Name? (brief)

Status: built 2026-10-26 as a prototype, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review.

- **Scope:** Kallimni 'Arabi Bishweesh, Module 1, Presentation 1 (greetings, introducing oneself, thanking,
  apologising, leave-taking; the pronoun suffixes on اسم). Our scenes and lines only.
- **Driving question:** why does the same question change when you ask a woman? (اسمَك → اسمِك)
- **Wow:** "put the answer where إيه was": the Egyptian question word sits at the end, in the slot of the answer.
- **Cast (running story of the beginner level):** Adam, a learner from abroad (teal); Amm Sayed the doorman (clay);
  Mona the neighbour (gold, hijab). Faceless, drawn in code. A Cairo building, stairs and a skyline as the accent.
- **Pages (14):** cover · the phrasebook problem · listen first (dialogue at the door) · greetings come in pairs · put
  the answer where إيه was · one letter makes a difference (Mona) · my name, your name · حضرتك · sorry, thank you ·
  goodbye · review (8 moments, ـَك or ـِك, reason after each) · role-play · mission · next (إنت منين؟).
- **Audio:** 38 clips in the Mahmoud Egyptian clone, listed with their emotion in `L01-clips.md`. Not generated yet:
  the ElevenLabs connector was not available in the 2026-10-26 session; each word shows "recording coming" until the
  file exists.

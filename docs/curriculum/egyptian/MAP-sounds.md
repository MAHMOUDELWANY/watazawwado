# Egyptian Arabic · Sounds (12+) · MAP

For learners who already know Fusha (Fusha Level 1 or more, or a school background) and want to understand and speak
Cairo Arabic fast. Scope: the sound and word shifts in *Egyptian Colloquial*, in our own order and scenes; every line is
ours (`egyptian/RULES.md` §2). Status: Claude's draft, 2026-11-04, for the owner's review. Plan:
`LEVELS-PLAN-2026-11.md` §2 (Sounds is 12+ only; children start at Beginner).

**The running story.** Adam (the Adults cast, teal) studied Fusha in London for two years. He lands in Cairo and moves
into the building with Amm Sayed the doorman and Mona the neighbour. Everyone understands his Fusha, and smiles at it.
Every lesson starts with something he says "like a book" and the Cairo version he hears back.

**How a lesson works.** A Fusha line and its Egyptian twin side by side, both playable (Mahmoud Fusha clone and the
owner's Egyptian recordings); the shift is shown as one sound that moves; then a word list where the learner predicts
the Egyptian form before hearing it; then exceptions (words that keep the Fusha sound); then a role-play.

| # | Lesson | Shift | Scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where Did the Qaf Go? | ق → ء (qalb → 'alb); ج → g (gamiil) | the kiosk and the coffee shop |
| 2 | Th and Dh | ث → t / s (talāta, sawra); ذ → d / z (dahab, zaki); ظ → z / d | the jeweller and the market |
| 3 | Short and Stressed | long vowels shorten in closed syllables; the Cairo stress (madrasa) | on the metro |
| 4 | Ay and Aw | ay → ee (beet), aw → oo (yoom) | Mona's family flat |
| 5 | The Question Words | ماذا → إيه، أين → فين، كيف → إزاي، متى → إمتى، لماذا → ليه | a lost tourist |
| 6 | Bi- and Ha- | present بيكتب, future هيكتب | a busy week |
| 7 | Ma…sh | negation: مبكتبش، مش | Adam's bad day |
| 8 | Review: Read Fusha, Say Egyptian | all shifts | a news headline read aloud at the café |

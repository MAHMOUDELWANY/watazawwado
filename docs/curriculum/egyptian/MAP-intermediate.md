# Egyptian Arabic · Intermediate · MAP (Adults, Teens, Kids)

Scope and order: the functions and structures of *Kallimni 'Arabi* (the intermediate book) and *Aktar*, re-told in our
own scenes (RULES.md). Status: Claude's draft, 2026-11-04, for the owner's review.

The learner finished Beginner: greets, asks, counts, shops, says what they do every day with بـ. Intermediate adds
time: what happened (the past), what will happen (هـ), and joining sentences into a story.

## Teens (12–17)

The summer club again, a year later: Karim, Salma and the team. Same structures, faster, with texting and voice notes.
1 "What Did You Do at the Match?" (the past, -t/-ti) · 2 "Tomorrow's Final" (هـ) · 3 "You Won't Believe What Happened"
(وبعدين، فجأة) · then the Adults order, with club scenes (a trip, a concert, a lost phone).

## Kids (6–11)

Back at Teta's flat with Ziad and Mariam. Shorter lessons (35–40 min), call-and-response games.
1 "Where Did You Go, Ziad?" (رحت / رحتي / راح / راحت) · 2 "Tomorrow We'll Go to the Zoo" (هـ) · 3 "Teta's Story"
(وبعدين). Then the Adults order, lighter: no reported speech or لو.

Notes
- The past-tense endings are taught as one rhythm (-t, -t, -ti, nothing, -it), the same pattern as the Fusha past the
  12+ Fusha learner already knows, with the vowels shortened (Sounds L3).
- Egyptian lines are recorded by the owner (no generated Egyptian voice).
- The Franco track follows the Adults lessons once they are reviewed (it plays the same recordings).

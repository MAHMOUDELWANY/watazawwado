# Egyptian Arabic · Beginner · Adults · MAP

Scope and order: the functions and structures of *Kallimni 'Arabi Bishweesh* (10 modules, two presentations each),
re-told in our own Cairo scenes (RULES.md). Status: Claude's draft, 2026-10-26, for the owner's review.

Running story: a learner from abroad moves into a flat in a Cairo building for a few months. The people of the building
and the street are the cast: Amm Sayed the doorman, Mona the neighbour, her family, the baker, the taxi driver.

Notes
- Literacy A comes first for learners who cannot read Arabic script; the Franco track uses the same map.
- The Fusha → Egyptian sound shifts (ق → ء، ث → ت/س، ذ → د/ز، ج → g) are taught where they first appear (L1: none;
  L2 جديد = gidiid), with a short "sound shift" note, never a separate lecture.

# Tajweed · Level 2 · Lesson 3 brief: The Deepest Tongue (ق ك)

Status: built 2026-11-03, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Content:
`src/curriculum/content/tajweed-2/l03-*`; graphics: `src/curriculum/slides/motion/g-tm3.tsx`.

- **Driving question:** قَلْب or كَلْب, قُلْ or كُلْ: one small step on the tongue decides the word. Where exactly is it?
- **Device:** a close-up of the back of the mouth (soft roof above, back of the tongue below). For ق the tongue rises to
  the furthest point and the mouth darkens (it fills: a heavy letter) with a bounce at a stop; for ك it touches one step
  forward and a puff of breath slips out (light). Then back to Lesson 1's map for the next stop.
- **Wow:** the voice and breath of the throat lesson return one step up: ق bounces, ك breathes.
- **Evidence:** Al-Ikhlas 112:4, كُفُوًا: the only time the word comes in the Quran; the surah opens with قُلْ.
- **Slips:** ق as k, ق as a hamza (Cairo's street ق, flagged for learners of our Egyptian track), ق as g, a heavy ك.
- **Practical:** sort 8 meanings and moments into ق or ك; recite Al-Ikhlas, 108:1 and 105:1.

# Tajweed · Level 1 · Lesson 1 brief: Every Letter Has a Right

Status: built 2026-10-26 as a prototype, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Version: one shared 12+
version on the Adults stage, with the Mushaf's green and gold as the subject accent.

- **Driving question:** if a letter can be said "right" and still be wrong (قَلْب / كَلْب), what does reciting the Quran
  well ask of us?
- **Wow:** the layers inside Al-Ikhlas: the bounce on the د that ends every ayah, the silent laam of ٱلصَّمَد, the merge in
  يَكُن لَّهُۥ; heard from the reciter, switched on one by one, each promised as a later lesson.
- **Practical:** isti'adha (16:98) and basmalah (every surah but At-Tawbah; optional mid-surah); recite Al-Ikhlas.
- **Review:** six mistakes, clear or hidden, with the reason.

# Tajweed · Level 3 · 12+ · MAP (advanced rules)

Scope from Jannat al-Quran *Tajweed* Book 4 (Book 3 is missing), checked against *Taysir ar-Rahman*; the order,
wording, examples and exercises are ours (`RULES.md` §3). Every count and rule is for **Hafs from Asim by way of
ash-Shatibiyyah**, as printed in the Mushaf of the King Fahd Complex; where Hafs has two allowed ways, both are taught.
Status: Claude's draft, 2026-11-03, for the owner's review. Plan: `LEVELS-PLAN-2026-11.md` §2.

**What Level 3 adds.** Level 1 named the rules a learner hears (basic madd, raa, the stopping signs). Level 3 makes a
reciter: every madd with its allowed counts and which one wins when two meet, where and how to stop and start, raa and
the heavy letters in all their cases, and the words where Hafs reads differently from what is written. The device for
the whole level is a **recitation ruler**: the counts drawn as lengths on one gold bar, so the learner sees every rule
as a measure, not a list.

**First three lessons** (as the levels plan set them): madd in full, stopping and starting, raa in full. Lessons 4–15
then go deeper into each.

## Notes

- Every ayah is fetched through the Quran connector and logged in each lesson brief, with a tadabbur stop; the
  reciter plays it. Nothing here is recited by a voice clone.
- Where Hafs by way of ash-Shatibiyyah allows two ways (the length of munfasil, فِرْقٍ, ضَعْف, ٱلْمُصَيْطِرُونَ), the
  lesson teaches both and the teacher keeps the learner on one.
- Ways that belong to other routes (for example a short munfasil, through at-Tayyibah) are mentioned only to say they
  are not ours here.
- The catalog entry `tajweed-3` (group `quran`, title "Tajweed · Level 3", version "Level 3") is added with Lesson 1.

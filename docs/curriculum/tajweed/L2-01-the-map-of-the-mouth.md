# Tajweed · Level 2 · Lesson 1 brief: The Map of the Mouth

Status: built 2026-11-03, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Version: one shared 12+ version on the
Adults stage, in Level 1's Mushaf green and gold. Map: `MAP-level-2.md`. Content:
`src/curriculum/content/tajweed-2/`; graphics: `src/curriculum/slides/motion/g-tm1.tsx`.

- **Driving question:** your teacher says "that letter was not right". What do you change, if you do not know where
  the letter is made?
- **The device for the whole level:** one side view of the mouth and throat (`MouthMap`). Areas light, a letter's point
  glows, and a bead of air rises from the throat and stops where a still letter is made. Anatomy only: no face.
- **Wow:** Al-Fatiha 1:2 tours four of the five areas in four words; its letters light on the map in reading order.
- **Method:** the hamza test (أَبْ، أَقْ، أَلْ), used in every lesson of the level.
- **Practical:** recite 1:2 word by word and name each letter's area; the review sorts 8 letters into throat, tongue,
  lips (result per area to the teacher's report).

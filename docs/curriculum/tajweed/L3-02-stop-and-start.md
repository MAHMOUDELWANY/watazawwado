# Tajweed · Level 3 · Lesson 2 brief: Stop and Start (al-waqf wa al-ibtida')

Status: built 2026-11-03, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Content:
`src/curriculum/content/tajweed-3/l02-*`; graphics: `src/curriculum/slides/motion/g-tr2.tsx`.

- **Driving question:** your breath runs out mid-ayah. Where may you stop, and where do you start again?
- **Device:** the ayah as a road of word tiles (right to left). A stop is a gate coloured by its kind; the words before
  it glow, the words after it wait; going back is a dotted arrow curving to the tile where the meaning starts; flags say
  "start here" or "not from here".
- **Hook and evidence:** Muhammad 47:19: stopping after لَآ إِلَـٰهَ leaves "there is no god". Tadabbur on فَٱعْلَمْ.
- **Content:** tamm (2:5, a new group in 2:6), kafi (2:6 to 2:7), hasan (ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ, go back; at an ayah's end the
  next ayah may start), qabih (4:43, shown as words only); ibtida' (start where the meaning starts); the Mushaf's signs
  مـ، لا، ج، صلى، قلى، ∴ (2:2, stop at one of the pair).
- **Practical:** sort 7 stopping places into "start with the next word", "go back, then start", "never stop here";
  recite Al-Baqarah 1-7 naming each stop.

# Tajweed · Level 2 · Lesson 2 brief: The Throat

Status: built 2026-11-03, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Content:
`src/curriculum/content/tajweed-2/l02-*`; graphics: `src/curriculum/slides/motion/g-tm2.tsx`.

- **Driving question:** four of the six throat letters sound alike to an English ear. How do you keep them apart?
- **Device:** the throat as a three-floor tower (the lowest floor nearest the chest). A bead of sound rises and stops on a
  floor; ء closes a door, ع and ح squeeze the walls, غ and خ ripple like a gargle; each letter card carries a sound wave
  (voice) or breath.
- **Wow:** every floor holds twins, one voiced (ء ع غ) and one breathed (ه ح خ): one rule tells all six apart (a preview
  of jahr and hams in Part B).
- **Evidence:** Al-Fatiha 1:5, نَعْبُدُ: said with a hamza it is no longer the revealed word; tadabbur on its root.
- **Practical:** sort 8 throat letters from real words into floors; tap the voiced letters; recite Al-Falaq and 1:2.

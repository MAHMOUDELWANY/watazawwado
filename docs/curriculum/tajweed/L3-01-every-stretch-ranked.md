# Tajweed · Level 3 · Lesson 1 brief: Every Stretch, Ranked

Status: built 2026-11-03, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Content:
`src/curriculum/content/tajweed-3/l01-*`; graphics: `src/curriculum/slides/motion/g-tr1.tsx` (the recitation ruler,
the device for the whole of Level 3). Counts: Hafs from Asim by way of ash-Shatibiyyah.

- **Driving question:** Level 1 gave each madd its own lesson. Seen side by side, how long is each one, and which one
  wins when you have to choose?
- **Device:** the recitation ruler: six counts as six marks; each madd a row whose bar fills to its length; a bead
  counts the beats; allowed choices (4 or 5; 2, 4 or 6) are rings on the track.
- **Content:** the two causes (a hamza or a sukoon after the madd letter); natural 2; muttasil and munfasil 4 or 5
  (keep one; muttasil never shorter than munfasil); lazim 6 (in a word and in a letter); 'aridh and leen at a stop
  2, 4 or 6; badal 2. The short munfasil of other routes is named only as "not our reading here".
- **Wow:** the ruler is also a ladder: lazim, muttasil, 'aridh, munfasil, badal.
- **Evidence:** Al-Baqarah 2:4 holds four kinds of madd in one ayah; tadabbur on يُوقِنُونَ.
- **Practical:** sort 8 stretches into 2, 4 or 5, and 6; tap the stretches that are always six; recite Al-Fatiha and
  Al-Baqarah 1-5 with the chosen lengths.

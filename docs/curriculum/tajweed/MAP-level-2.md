# Tajweed · Level 2 · 12+ · MAP (makharij and sifat)

Scope from Jannat al-Quran *Tajweed* Book 2 (the makharij and sifat chapters), checked against *Taysir ar-Rahman*;
the order, wording, examples and exercises are ours (`RULES.md` §3). Status: Claude's draft, 2026-11-03, for the owner's
review. Plan: `LEVELS-PLAN-2026-11.md` §2.

**What Level 2 adds.** Level 1 taught the rules a learner hears while reciting. Level 2 answers *where* each letter is
born (makhraj) and *how* it comes out (sifah), so the learner can fix a letter instead of only hearing that it is wrong.
The count follows the common view in Hafs by way of ash-Shatibiyyah: **5 areas, 17 points** (Ibn al-Jazari).

**How the lessons work.** One animated side view of the mouth and throat runs through the whole level: each lesson
lights up one area, the letter's point glows, and the air moves the way the letter is said. Every letter is found the
same way: a hamza with a vowel before it and a sukoon on it (أَبْ، أَقْ). The reciter plays the ayah; the learner hears
the letter before naming its place. Every ayah is fetched through the Quran connector, logged in the lesson brief and
has a tadabbur stop. The faces drawn are anatomy, never a person.

## Part A · Where letters are born (makharij)

| # | Lesson | What it teaches | Letters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Map of the Mouth | the five areas (jawf, halq, lisan, shafatan, khayshum); how to find any letter's point | all, as a map |
| 2 | The Throat | three depths of the throat: deepest, middle, nearest | ء ه · ع ح · غ خ |
| 3 | The Deepest Tongue | the back of the tongue against the soft palate, and the step between them | ق ك |
| 4 | The Middle of the Tongue | the middle of the tongue to the roof of the mouth | ج ش ي |
| 5 | The Side of the Tongue | the hardest letter of Arabic, and the edge that makes laam | ض · ل |
| 6 | The Tip, Part 1 | the tip near the gums: three close neighbours | ن · ر |
| 7 | The Tip, Part 2 | the tip on the root of the upper front teeth | ط د ت |
| 8 | The Whistle | the tip near the lower teeth, air through a narrow gap | ص ز س |
| 9 | Between the Teeth | the tip between the teeth, and the mistake of saying z or s | ظ ذ ث |
| 10 | The Lips | the lower lip and upper teeth; both lips | ف · ب م و |
| 11 | The Empty Space and the Nose | the madd letters from the jawf; ghunnah from the khayshum | ا و ي (madd) · ghunnah |
| 12 | Review: Every Letter Has a Home | all 17 points on one map, letters sorted by ear | all |

## Notes

- **First three lessons** (`LEVELS-PLAN` §2): 1, 2, 3 above. Practice ayat are chosen in each brief from Juz 'Amma and
  Al-Fatiha and verified there, never in this map.
- Lessons 5 (ض) and 9 (ظ ذ ث) carry the mistakes English speakers make most; each gets a "say it wrong, then right"
  page with the reciter.
- The catalog entry `tajweed-2` (group `quran`, title "Tajweed · Level 2", version "Level 2") is added with Lesson 1.
- Level 3 (advanced rules) is mapped separately in `MAP-level-3.md`; Level 1 already holds basic madd, raa and the
  stopping signs, so Level 3 goes deeper on those.

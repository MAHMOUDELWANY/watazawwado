# Style guide — Zad Path · Kids (6–11)

Binding for every image in a Kids lesson (`docs/curriculum/RULES.md` §1.10). Taken from the owner's Lesson 1 paintings
(`public/curriculum/zad-kids/l01/`, generated by the owner in ChatGPT, 2026-10-17). The character sheet
(`characters.webp`) is the reference: when an image disagrees with it, the sheet wins.

## Look
- Warm, painterly children's-book illustration (soft gouache/oil texture, visible brush grain), not flat vector, not 3D.
- Golden-hour light: amber, honey, terracotta; deep teal and crimson as accents. Night scenes are warm lamp-lit interiors
  with a deep blue window.
- Brand palette in the scene where it fits: crimson `#C51F24`, teal `#087D91`, copper `#8B4935`, gold `#E5A93C`.
- Islamic geometric details (khatam star, mihrab arch, kilim and tile patterns) in cushions, rugs, frames and lanterns.
- Plain cream background for character and object studies.
- Every image teaches: one clear subject, room left for the scene build (labels, pins, reveals).

## Characters (fixed, from the character sheet)

**Known mismatch to fix:** Maryam's hijab is teal in three Lesson 1 paintings and gold in the sheet. Gold is canonical;
regenerate or recolour those images when they are next touched.

## Never
- No faces or bodies of prophets, Sahaba or Jibril. Show the place and the light; Jibril only as the white-clothed figure
  seen from behind at a distance (as in `madinah.webp`, `stranger.webp`).
- No text inside generated images (labels are added by the slide).
- No music; sound effects only.

## Base prompt (prefix every Kids image prompt with this)
> Warm painterly children's-book illustration, soft gouache texture, golden-hour light, palette of amber, terracotta,
> deep teal and crimson, Islamic geometric patterns in textiles and decor, 4:3 landscape, no text in the image.

Then add the characters exactly as in the table above, the scene, and what must be visible for the lesson's scene build.
Save as WebP in `public/curriculum/zad-kids/lNN/` and record it in `docs/curriculum/sources.md`.

**Look in practice (2026-10-18):** the owner's Lesson 2 paintings came out as soft 3D animated-film illustration rather
than gouache, and the owner accepted them. New images match that set: in ChatGPT, attach `characters.webp` and one
Lesson 2 painting (`l02/crash.webp`) as references and add "same style and same characters as the reference images".

## Three kinds of images (RULES.md §1.10)

| Kind | What | Background | Folder | Reuse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scene | A 4:3 painting of a place or moment | Painted | `lNN/` | One page per lesson only |
| Character | One character, one pose, full body | **Transparent PNG** | `characters/` | Every lesson |
| Prop | One object that must move on its own | **Transparent PNG** | `lNN/` | The lesson that needs it |

**Character / prop prompt (prefix):**
> Same style and same character as the reference images. A single [character / object], full body, centred, soft studio
> light from the upper left, a gentle shadow under the feet, **transparent background (PNG)**, nothing else in the
> image, no text.

If ChatGPT returns a background anyway, ask for "plain pure white background" instead; Claude removes it with ffmpeg.

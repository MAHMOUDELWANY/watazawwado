# Literacy C · Reading Franco · Adults · MAP (Claude's draft, 2026-11-02, for the owner's review)

Rules: `franco/RULES.md`. One version (adults). Seven lessons, then the learner moves to Egyptian · Franco.
Running scene: **Tant Hoda**, the learner's aunt in Cairo, starts texting on the family WhatsApp group. Each lesson the
learner can read more of her messages; the last lesson is a whole conversation with her, read and answered.

| # | Lesson | Sounds and rules | The learner can... |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Numbers That Talk | why Franco; the plain letters; **3** (ع) and **7** (ح) | read a greeting with 3 and 7 and know why a number stands for a sound |
| 2 | The Cairo Hamza | **2** for ء and for Cairo's ق; the article **el** | read questions and words with 2 (2alb, so2al, el 2ahwa) |
| 3 | Scrape and Gargle | **5** (خ), **gh** (غ), **sh**, Cairo **g** (ج) | read food, family and weather words |
| 4 | Long and Short | short a e o; long aa ee oo; doubled letters | tell beet from bet, read names and doubled words |
| 5 | Heavy Letters | t s d z for ط ص ض ظ; ث and ذ as Cairo says them | read words where the spelling hides a heavy sound |
| 6 | Little Words | w, el, ya, ma3a, men, fi; the endings -ak -ek -i | read whole sentences fast |
| 7 | Tant Hoda's Voice Note | everything | read a full chat and answer it in Franco |

Ending: the learner answers Tant Hoda's long message alone, then starts Egyptian · Franco Lesson 1.

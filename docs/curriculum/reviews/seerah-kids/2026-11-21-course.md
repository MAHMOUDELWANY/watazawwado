## Verdict

**Accumulation does not work. The four lessons are four separate stories that happen to be in order.** Each
cliffhanger is picked up by the next lesson's first story page, so the plot holds. Nothing else is carried. Across four
lessons there is **one** on-slide callback to an earlier episode (L3 p9, the camel), and it answers its own question in
the same sentence. The child never has to *remember* anything: zero retrieval in 55 pages. Three of three weekly
commitments are never asked about. Abdul-Muttalib, planted in L1 with the note "The child will meet him again", is
reintroduced in L3 as if new. Aminah is named for the first time in L2's teaser and dies three pages into L3. His
father is never named. L4 does not mention one thing from L1 or L2. On top of that the design system works against the
web: the cast guide Yusuf stands beside the line about the Prophet ﷺ as a boy on 18 pages, and Jiddo stands in for
Abdul-Muttalib, Bahira and Abu Talib on 6. The fix is small and mechanical: one "Story so far" page per lesson,
about 15 named callback lines, a fixed age ribbon, and the cast moved out of the story frame onto the asking pages,
where it gets a job.

### course-2 · major · confirmed
where: course (L2 p0–p3, L3 p0–p10, L4 p0–p11)
evidence: Ledger above: 10 references, 1 named on a slide (L3 p9), 0 retrieval. L4 refers to nothing in L1 or L2. ### course-5 · major · judgement
where: course (all four lessons; template in `docs/curriculum/seerah/RULES.md`)
evidence: The Seerah episode template has Chapter pages with an I'tizaz line, an Interlude, a Quote card, a Stamp (date and place) and a two-mystery cliffhanger. In Kids L1–L4: no I'tizaz line on any story page (the carry appears only on mission pages: L2 p12, L3 p12, L4 p12; L1 has none: L1 curriculum-designer-1); no Stamp in any lesson (L1 subject-scholar-7); every cliffhanger has one mystery or none (L1 story-editor-7, L2 story-editor-12, L3 story-editor-4, L4 story-editor-5). The four lessons follow his age from birth to twelve, and nothing on screen shows it.
why: The Kids band took the generic Zad Kids page set and dropped the parts of the Seerah template that make a *series*: the Stamp and the cliffhanger are exactly the devices that tie episode to episode. For 6–11, a visible timeline is the strongest single organiser of a chronological story (an advance organiser, Ausubel 1960): the child sees where this episode sits and what came before.
fix: One age ribbon on every cover and on the "Story so far" page: a strip of dots "born · 4–5 · 6 · 8 · 12" with past dots filled and an icon for each (elephant, donkey, lamp, rug, caravan); it grows by one dot per episode. One I'tizaz line per lesson on the story page where the person acts (L1 p5: "Makkah was small. Small is not the same as wrong."). Cliffhangers to two concrete questions, as the lesson findings above give.

## The rule for every future lesson

**No person, place, sign or ayah comes back without its episode, and the child says it first.** Every Kids Seerah lesson
from Episode 2 on opens, after the cover, with a "Story so far" page that asks last week's Ahd by name and one fact
from an older episode. Every return later in the lesson is written as "Remember … in Episode N?", asked on one step,
answered on the next. The cast lives only on these asking pages, never inside the story. (Checkable in `audit.ts`: page
1 of L≥2 is `spark/question`; at least two lines per lesson match "Episode \d".)

## Keep

- Every cliffhanger is picked up on the next lesson's first story page; the plot chain L1→L4 holds.
- The slow donkey: planted in L1 p12, set up in L2 p2, paid off in L2 p7. The model for every thread.
- L3 p9's line "Wherever this boy went, blessings came too": keep the sentence, change the question before it.
- "It is said" marking on the reported details (L3 p8, L4 p4–p5) and the source per page in the notes.
- One painting per page with no figure of the Prophet ﷺ: the paintings already obey the rule the cast breaks.

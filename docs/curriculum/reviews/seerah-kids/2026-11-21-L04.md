### age-band-1 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L4 p3 s0–s2, p8 s2, teacher notes p3/p8 (interfaith framing)
- **evidence:** p3: "Near Busra lived a monk called Bahira. ### learning-scientist-1 · confirmed · status: open
- **where:** L4 p0–p1 (Spark) and the whole lesson; block end L1–L4
- **evidence:** The only backward link in L4 is p0 explain: `"Episode 4!" Remind them: Uncle Abu Talib was going on a big trip, and the boy said "Take me with you!"` — the teacher re-tells L3's cliffhanger. No page or card asks the child to recall anything from L1 (Year of the Elephant), L2 (Halimah, the blessings in her tent) or L3 (Aminah, Grandpa, Abu Talib taking him in, the rain). L3 p9 had set up a thread: "Wherever this boy went, blessings came too." L4 p4–p5 (cloud, leaning tree) are the next link in that thread and say nothing about it.
- **why:** Re-telling is not retrieval. Pulling an item back from memory strengthens it far more than hearing it again (testing effect: Roediger & Karpicke 2006), and the gain grows when retrieval is spaced across sessions (Cepeda et al. 2006). L4 closes the first block, the natural spacing point, and spends it on zero retrieval. The blessing thread is exactly the elaborative link that ties four episodes into one memory (elaborative interrogation: Pressley et al. 1987); here it is left for the child to notice alone.
- **fix:** Add a Spark page before p1, "Do you remember?", `kind: 'ask'` in three quick steps, the child answers before each reveal: "Who looked after the boy after Grandpa died?" (Uncle Abu Talib — needed for this episode); "What happened to Halimah's thin camel and slow donkey?" (they became strong: blessing); "What happened when Abu Talib prayed for rain with the boy beside him?" (it rained). On p4 add: "Do you remember Halimah's tent and the rain in Makkah? Here is another one…", then the cloud.
- **refuter:** The only backward link is the p0 "Remind them" retelling; no page or card asks the child to recall L1–L3, and the blessing thread from L3 is not picked up on p4–p5.

### learning-scientist-1 · confirmed · status: open
- **where:** L1 p9 s1 (desktop, all viewports)
- **evidence:** `l01-the-elephant-who-sat-down.ts` L163–168 authors a `why` for every card ("It would not walk toward the Kaaba.", "Allah protected it."). The Kids path renders `SortGame` in `src/curriculum/slides/Slide.tsx` L941–1068 (sk1Sort has no on-stage entry in `cinema/scenes.tsx`, so it falls through to `InteractionView` L794). `SortGame` never reads `card.why` (only `motion/Questions.tsx` L165 does). A wrong tap gets only "Not that door. Look again and try another one."; the end screen gets "All sorted! n of 6 on the first try."
- **why:** Feedback that explains why an answer is right or wrong works better than a bare right/wrong signal (Shute 2008, "Focus on formative feedback", Rev. Educ. Res.; Hattie & Timperley 2007). With two doors, "try another one" means the second tap is always right, so the child learns to click the other door rather than to recall the story. The written explanations are lost.
- **fix:** In `SortGame`, after any drop (right, or the second try after a miss), show `cards[current].why` under the card for about 2 s before the next card comes up (e.g. "Not in the story: Makkah had no army at all."). Lock both doors during a miss and show the `why`, so the child cannot just click the other door. Show the first-try miss list with each `why` on the done screen.
- **refuter:** Slide.tsx SortGame (L941-1068) never reads card.why (only motion/Questions.tsx L165 does), and a miss only says "Not that door... try another one".

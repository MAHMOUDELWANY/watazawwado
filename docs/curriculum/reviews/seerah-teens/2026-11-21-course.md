# Course panel · seerah-teens L1–L4 · 2026-11-21

## Verdict

**Accumulation does not work. The four Teens episodes tell one story in order, but the learner never has to carry
anything from one to the next.** The plot chain holds from L2 on (L2→L3 and L3→L4 cliffhangers are paid off on the
next lesson's story pages), and L3 has the two best links in either band ("Count the hands that held him… Halimah in
the desert" and "A dry camel in the desert. A dry valley in Makkah"). Everything else is missing. Across 79 pages:
**0 retrieval** (the learner never produces an earlier fact), **0 slide lines that name an earlier episode**, **0 of 3
Ahd follow-ups**, 20 references in all, of which 5 live only in teacher notes and 14 are told or assumed on the slide.
The first cliffhanger contradicts its payoff ("two figures in white… what do they place inside?" against one Jibril who
takes a clot out). Aminah is not named in L1 and dies three pages into L3; Abdul-Muttalib, the hero of L1, is absent
from L2 and comes back in L3 as a stranger; the Kaaba of L1 stands behind three L3 pages without a word. The four
lessons each state a two-half formula, and three of them are the same idea (your part + Allah's part), but no lesson
says so. The cinematic deck has no part built for a series: no "Previously", no age stamp that grows, no map that
grows. On top, the image generator keeps putting the modern Kaaba, Tuscan Busra and a lone robed figure into the
6th century, because the self-contained LOOK T line carries none of the era clauses the Kids line carries.

**Teens against Kids.** Slightly better: twice the told references (20 against 10), the hands-count and the camel link,
and no code default that puts a cast member in the Prophet's ﷺ place (Kids course-1). Not better where it matters:
the same zero retrieval, the same 0 of 3 Ahd, the same family never placed in L1, the same unlinked Kaaba and the same
broken blessing thread at L4. Worse in one place: the L1→L2 and L2→L3 teasers contradict their payoff, where Kids'
chain held. Shared defects are stated once here and in Kids course-2 to course-6; the fix is the same for both bands
(one rule at the end).

## Findings

### course-1 · major · confirmed
where: course (L2 p0–p5, L3 p0–p14, L4 p0–p11; the four check pages L1 p17, L2 p17, L3 p17, L4 p15)
evidence: Ledger above: 20 references, 0 retrieval, 0 slide lines naming an earlier episode; 0 of 24 check cards test an earlier episode; every mission page ends "Ask about it next episode" (L1 p19, L2 p18, L3 p18, L4 p16) and no next-lesson Spark, slide or Arabic note asks it. Lesson panels found the pieces: L1 story-editor-6, L2 curriculum-designer-1, L2 curriculum-designer-6, L3 curriculum-designer-2, L3 learning-scientist-1, L3 learning-scientist-3, L3 teacher-parent-1, L4 curriculum-designer-3, L4 learning-scientist-1. Same pattern and same cause as Kids course-2 and Kids course-3.
why: The owner's first content complaint is "a later lesson that never says 'as we mentioned in Lesson 1'". Teens tells more than Kids, and telling is the weak form: spaced retrieval, where the learner produces the earlier fact, is what makes it stick (Roediger & Karpicke 2006; Cepeda et al. 2006). A weekly one-to-one series is built for it and uses none. The Ahd follow-up is written into the wrong file: the teacher opening L3 reads L3's notes, not L2's last page; RULES §1.5 puts the check in the next Spark for that reason.
fix: Page 1 of L2, L3 and L4 becomes "Previously" (`station: 'spark'`, `layout: 'question'`, `interaction: ask`): last week's Ahd quoted by name, then one fact from the last episode and one from an older one, each answered before the reveal (exact lines in the per-lesson changes). Delete "Ask about it next episode" from every mission page. Every later return in a lesson is asked on one step and answered on the next, with "Episode N" on the slide.

### course-2 · minor · confirmed
where: course (L1 p18, L2 p13, L2 p15, L2 p19, L3 p1, L3 p9, L4 p2, L4 p9)
evidence: His age is shown four different ways and never carried: L1 p18 an item card "571 CE · the Year of the Elephant"; L2 p13 a timeline "0 · 2 · 4–5" (L2 art-director-5: 10 px labels); L3 p9 a count-up "8" (L3 motion-defects-1); L4 no device, "about twelve" (p2) and "the twelve-year-old" (p9) in text only. The contradictions this lets through were raised one by one: L2 p15 "forty years later" (L2 subject-scholar-3), L2 p19 "about six" against L3 p1 "Two years together" (L2 subject-scholar-2, L3 story-editor-2), L1 p20 "Years later" for age 4–5 (L1 subject-scholar-9). Nothing bridges eight (L3 p9) to twelve (L4 p2). Kids course-5 found the same gap in Kids.
why: A chronological series needs one fixed organiser the learner sees grow (Ausubel's advance organiser); with four devices, nobody, writer included, checks one episode's age against the next, which is how three age errors reached the page. The 40-year-old convert, who does not know the order of the Seerah, loses the most.
fix: One "stamp" component (the deck's own Stamp page kind) on every cover and on the "Previously" page: a strip "571 · born — 2 — 4–5 — 6 — 8 — 12" with past points filled and this episode's point lit; it gains one point per episode. Replace the L2 p13 timeline and the L3 p9 count-up with it. L4 p2 line 1 carries the bridge (per-lesson changes).

### course-6 · major · judgement
where: course (the Teens episode template; L1 p18, p20; L2 p13; L3 p2, p9; L4 p3; `docs/curriculum/seerah/RULES.md` episode template)
evidence: The look is right: dark stills, gold serif, three short lines per page work for a 13-year-old and do not talk down to a 40-year-old. But the system is built for one episode. The RULES template lists Chapter, Interlude, Integrated lesson, Quote card, Quran verdict, Stamp and Cliffhanger; it has no page kind for "Previously", though every cover calls itself "Episode N". The Stamp is rendered as an ordinary card (L1 art-director-16); the two-mystery cliffhanger as the same three-line layout as every page (L1 art-director-16); 19 of 21 L1 pages share one composition (L1 art-director-5). The maps do not grow: L1 none, L3 a journey map, L4 p3 a map with two dots, "Makkah" and "Busra", that omits the Yathrib road of L3 (L4 art-director-4). The quote card and the formula page are the only recurring parts, and neither refers to an earlier episode.
why: A drama series holds its audience across weeks with three devices: "previously on", a visible place in the timeline, and a map that fills in. The deck copies the series look and leaves out all three, so the design itself gives the writer no slot for a callback. That is why the told references in the ledger sit in odd lines and notes: there is nowhere else to put them.
fix: Add three components to the Teens template and to RULES: a "Previously" page (ask layout: Ahd check, two recall asks, one earlier still dimmed behind it), the stamp strip of course-2 on every cover, and one route map component that keeps every earlier episode's places as grey dots labelled "Episode N" and adds this episode's in gold (L3 p2 and L4 p3 first). The cliffhanger uses the deck's two framed mystery cards and "Coming next".

### course-7 · major · judgement

## The rule for every future lesson

**No person, place, word, sign or ayah comes back without its episode, and the learner says it first.** From Episode 2
on, page 1 is "Previously": last week's Ahd by name, then one fact from the last episode and one from an older one, each
asked before the reveal. Every later return is written "Remember … in Episode N?" on the slide, asked on one step and
answered on the next. The same rule as Kids, so the subject has one. Checkable in `scripts/curriculum/audit.ts`: page 1
of every lesson ≥ 2 is `spark` + `question`; at least two learner lines per lesson match `Episode \d` outside the
teaser; no mission page `explain` contains "next episode".

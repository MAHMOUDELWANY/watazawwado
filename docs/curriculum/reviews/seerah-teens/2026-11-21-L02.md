## Major (14)

### age-band-1 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L2 title, p1 l4, p14 l2 (and the whole premise)
- **evidence:** title "The Desert Nurse"; p1 l4 "bedouin women came to Makkah looking for babies to nurse"; p14 l2 "His foster sister ash-Shayma' used to carry him"; p9 l1 "Halimah found she had milk". No line or `explain` says what wet-nursing is, or what a milk sibling is.
- **why:** The episode rests on a practice neither learner knows. To T, "nurse" is a hospital worker and "foster" means foster care (a child taken from their family by social services); "The Desert Nurse" reads as a medical title. A may know "wet nurse" from period drama but not that nursing creates a milk family in Islamic law (mahram ties, the word رضاعة), which is the one adult-level idea this episode naturally carries. Fails both. The teacher, possibly of the other gender to a teen, gets no wording for the breastfeeding content.
- **fix:** p1 add l2: "This was called rada'ah (رضاعة): a 'milk mother' from the desert breastfed the baby, often for two years, and her family became his milk family." p14 l2: "His milk sister ash-Shayma'". Title stays, subtitle on the cover: "His milk mother, Halimah". Teacher note p1/p9 (EN + AR): "Say it plainly and briefly: she fed him with her own milk, as mothers do. In Islam this makes a real family tie: milk siblings may not marry. No more detail is needed."
- **refuter:** No line or explain says what a wet nurse or a milk sibling is, and "nurse" and "foster sister" mislead a 13-year-old.

### learning-scientist-2 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L2 p12 (integrated lesson) with p9–p11
- **evidence:** p12 l3 "That is the pattern of this whole series: we do the good thing, and Allah decides how big it grows."; items "Your part: the kind choice" / "Allah's part: the barakah"; p9–p11 show only material rewards (milk, camel, donkey, grass). MAP row 2: "Blessing follows the one who chooses kindness." No page shows a kind choice with no visible return.
- **why:** Concept teaching needs examples and non-examples to bound the concept (Tennyson & Cocchiarella, 1986, *Review of Educational Research* 56:40; Merrill, *First Principles of Instruction*, 2002). With four material-reward examples and no boundary case, the learner will generalise "kindness → visible reward soon". That is the misconception the lesson must prevent: the first time a teen's kindness is ignored, or an adult's charity is followed by loss, the rule fails and takes the lesson's credibility with it.
- **fix:** Add one line and one case. p12 after l2: "Barakah is not always milk and grass. Sometimes it is peace, a child who turns out well, or a reward you only see in the Hereafter." Add a case item to the check (curriculum-designer-4): "You helped someone and nothing good came back. Was it wasted?" with the reason "Your part was done. Allah's part may come in another form or another life."
- **refuter:** p9–p11 show only material rewards and p12 l3 states "we do the good thing, and Allah decides how big it grows" with no boundary case.
- **also raised by:** age-band-2

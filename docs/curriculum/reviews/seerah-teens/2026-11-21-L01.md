### age-band-1 · confirmed · status: open
- **where:** L1 p1–p20, every stage (desktop, all viewports)
- **evidence:** `src/curriculum/slides/motion/g-se1.tsx` L124–146: each page shows one gold Arabic term and nothing else: `'التَّوَكُّل'` (p11), `'اخْتِبَارُ المَنْطِق'` (p6), `'وَهْمُ القُوَّة'` (p13), `'عِزَّةُ الحَقّ'` (p16), `'المَوْلِدُ النَّبَوِيّ'` (p18), `'زَادِي'` (p19). Seen on `L1/steps/p01.png` (`عَالَمُ الإِمْبِرَاطُورِيَّات`, no transliteration, no meaning) and `p02.png` (`الجَاهِلِيَّة`, the only one the English lines gloss). The English text never says "tawakkul": p11 reads only "True trust" and "Half two: 100% surrender", while outcome 3 is "Explains tawakkul as full effort plus full surrender".
- **why:** The key-term device exists to give the learner one Arabic word per episode to keep. A cannot read Arabic script at all; T can usually sound out letters but not the meaning of a phrase like اختبار المنطق. A term with no transliteration and no meaning is decoration to both, and the lesson's most important concept (tawakkul) never reaches the English learner as a word they could say back. Several terms are not terms but invented headlines (وهم القوة, اختبار المنطق), which teach nothing and train A to skip the Arabic.
- **fix:** Under every gold term, a small line: transliteration · meaning, e.g. "at-tawakkul · trusting Allah after doing your part"; "al-Jahiliyyah · the age of ignorance"; "'izzat al-haqq · the dignity of the truth". Replace the headline phrases on p6 and p13 with real vocabulary from the story (p6 "jaysh wa bayt · an army and a House"; p13 "baraka · it knelt"). On p11 change line 1 to "This is tawakkul (التوكل): Abdul-Muttalib did not throw stones at an army…".
- **refuter:** g-se1.tsx shows each gold Arabic term with no transliteration or meaning, and "tawakkul" never appears in the English lines.

### age-band-2 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L1 p11 (item cards) against p6 (ask)
- **evidence:** p6 asks "what do you do: give up, or hold your ground?"; five pages later p11's card says "Half two: 100% surrender" (`l01-the-invincible-army.ts`, `items: ['Half one: 100% effort', 'Half two: 100% surrender']`).
- **why:** In a Western 13-year-old's English, "surrender" means giving up, losing, the white flag. The lesson sets up "give up" as the wrong answer on p6 and then names the right answer "100% surrender" on p11: T hears a contradiction.

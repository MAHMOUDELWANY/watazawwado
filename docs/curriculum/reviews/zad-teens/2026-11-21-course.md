### course-1 · major · confirmed
where: course (L2 p1, p2, p13; L3 p1, p2, p6, p13; all three games)
evidence: See the callback ledger. A grep of `l03-one-not-many.ts` returns 0 hits for "Iman", "door", "six", "Ihsan", "Jibril", "stranger", "fathers", "'aql" and "watching". The only "Lesson 1" in L3 is teacher text (p6 explain, line 177: "That links straight back to Lesson 1"). L2 names no earlier lesson on any slide; its only L1 content is the p1 recap "A stranger with no dust. Three doors: Iman, Islam, Ihsan." Both recall pages print the answers before the ask (`L2/steps/p01.png` tiles 2–4, `L3/steps/p01.png` tiles 3–6; already raised as L2 learning-scientist-1 and L3 learning-scientist-1). The 24 game cards (8 per lesson) all come from the lesson they sit in.
why: The owner's question is whether learning accumulates. What the course has is a chain: each lesson nods only to the lesson before it, and every nod is told, never recalled. Memory builds a web only when earlier material is retrieved from memory in a new setting: retrieval practice (Roediger & Karpicke 2006), spacing (Cepeda et al. 2006), and linking new material to what the learner already knows (Ausubel 1960). By L3, everything L1 taught is gone from the screen. The Why List drawing is the one thing that carries from lesson to lesson (course-2).
fix: Make the line edits in "Accumulation design" below: about 22 lines, prompts and cards, and no new pages. Adopt the Teens web rule (course-12). The general web rule in Adults course-14 is still missing from `docs/curriculum/RULES.md` §1.5 and needs adding there too.

### course-2 · major · confirmed
where: course (L1 p16; L2 p1, p11, p13, p14; L3 p1, p13, p14; `WhyList` in t01.tsx L19)
evidence:
- The L1 brief's Ahd says the learner "writes it on their own Why List". The L1 p16 drawing adds a learner row, "Your question: …" (t01.tsx L373: `rows={[Q1, 'Your question: …']}`).
- The L2 p1 reveal promises: "Keep it. We will cross questions off one by one, and today we cross off one of Zayn's." The L2 p11 note says to "note them for the Why List".
- From L2 on, the drawn list holds only Zayn's lines: `rows={[Q1, Q2]}` (t02.tsx L336) and `[Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4]` (t03.tsx L239). The learner's row is gone (`L2/steps/p13.png` tile 4; `L3/steps/p13.png` tile 2).
- No later page or note asks for the learner's question again, routes it to a lesson, or crosses it off.
why: This is the course's own accumulation device, and it accumulates the wrong person's questions. The MAP's engine is "burning curiosity: the learner talks, asks and argues back". The list is drawn beautifully and stacks one row per lesson, but it never holds the one question that is the learner's. A promise on a slide ("we will cross questions off") that is never kept teaches a teen that the course does not mean what it says. That is the same failure as L1 story-editor-1, now at course scale.
fix:
- Give `WhyList` a fixed learner row ("Your question") under Zayn's rows, on every page where the list appears. It holds the text the learner typed or chose on the Ahd card; if the player cannot store it yet, the teacher types it.
- L2 p1 reveal → "Keep it on your list. When a lesson answers it, you cross it off yourself."
- Add to every recall note: "Read the learner's open questions. Tell them which lesson in the map answers each one, and add that lesson's chip to their row (as 'Lesson 11' on Zayn's first line)."
- When a lesson answers a learner's question, they cross it off on the Ahd page, next to Zayn's.

**The Teens web rule (for the curriculum-builder, every lesson from Lesson 2)**
1. **Recall page, one template:** the Why List opens (Zayn's rows plus the learner's row, last week's line crossed off) beside the three doors with today's door lit. One closed-book question on the last lesson and one on a lesson two or more back, answered before anything shows. Then the habit check, naming every option from last week.
2. **Zad:** at least two slide lines name an earlier lesson by number ("Lesson 1's…") and add something new to it, plus at least one ask answered from memory. A link that appears only in `explain` does not count.
3. **Characters:** every question a character asks is answered to that character on a slide, in that lesson, or carries a visible chip naming the lesson that will. Hamza carries one reasoning step per lesson; every character from the opening speaks at least once per Part.
4. **Game:** at least two cards from earlier lessons.
5. **Ahd:** one option grows last week's option; the next Spark names every option.
6. **Terms:** the MAP glossary is used unchanged (the three doors, the three ways, the Why List, Ihsan's one gloss). The brief gets a "Callbacks" table (lesson, page, what is recalled, ask or line), filled before the page text is written and checked by the course panel (Adults course-14).

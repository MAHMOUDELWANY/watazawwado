### age-band-2 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L1 p2 s3 (teacher note) and p2 choice
- **evidence:** slide: "Be honest: what is your faith mostly built on right now?" with the option "I have never really thought about it". Teacher note: "Note their answer in your report." (Arabic note: «سجّل إجابته في التقرير»). zad-essentials-teens/RULES.md §2: "The teacher can pass a note to the family in the report."
- **why:** emotional safety and trust. A teen is asked to "be honest" about the weakest spot in their faith, and the answer
then goes into a report that is the channel to the family. If a parent reads "has never really thought about it", the
teen gets a talk at home, and learns in Lesson 1 that honesty here gets reported. Adolescents disclose only where they
expect confidentiality (judgement; a standard principle of youth work and of school counselling). The whole course runs on
the learner saying "I don't know" and "why?" out loud, so this one note can undo that.
- **fix:** teacher note: "Keep their answer in your private teacher notes, not in the family report. Tell them: 'This stays
between us. It just helps me know where we're starting.'" Apply the same rule to every "be honest" choice in the course.
(Not checked: whether the lesson report is actually visible to parents in the portal.)
- **refuter:** p2 teacherNote really sends the learner's "be honest" faith answer to the report, and Teens RULES §2 makes the report a family channel.

### learning-scientist-1 · confirmed · status: open
- **where:** L1 p7 s2 (desktop)

### learning-scientist-2 · confirmed · status: open
- **where:** L1 p4 s1–s6 (desktop); p3 s3
- **evidence:** p4 lines: "So a clean traveller would have to be a local." / "But Madinah was a small town where everybody knew everybody, and nobody knew him." The evidence meaning underlines exactly "no sign of travel" and "none of us knew who he was" (steps/p04.png tile 6, red underlines). - **why:** Three faults in one task. (c) "He wore white" is marked wrong although p3 primed the white clothes as the strange detail, and very white clothes are part of why he showed "no sign of travel". A learner who followed p3 is told they are wrong for it: incorrect feedback on a correct inference. The stage drawing the "CONTRADICTION" stamp before the card is CW-4 (listed below).
- **fix:** Remove lines 3–4 from `lines` and show them as the feedback after both correct taps. Remove the underlines until the task is solved. Replace the clues with four facts that each tempt: "No sign of travel on him" (correct), "Nobody knew who he was" (correct), "He sat very close to the Prophet ﷺ", "He asked about Islam". Pay off p3 in the feedback: "His spotless white clothes and black hair are the proof: no dust, no tired road on him."

### subject-scholar-7 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L1 p3 `madinah.webp`, p4 `stranger.webp`, p7 `courtyard.webp`, p10–p11 `walk-away.webp` (rule: zad-essentials-teens/RULES.md §4, "Six-pointed star patterns and minarets in images are fine (owner, 2026-10-21)")

- **why:** The rule is right about decorative patterns, but minarets here are a historical error. The Masjid of the Prophet ﷺ had no minaret in his lifetime: Bilal called the adhan from a high place, and minarets were added in a later generation (I am not stating a year without a source). The crescent finial and domes belong to much later Islamic architecture. Madinah also had no city wall then; the trench of al-Khandaq was dug because it had none. Here the images claim to show "one day in Madinah" in the Prophet's time, beside the narration of an eyewitness who remembered every detail, so they are evidence-adjacent and not decoration. A teacher who knows the seerah, or a parent with a seerah book, will catch it. Lesson 4 ("Can I Trust Him?") and later seerah lessons will reuse this Madinah.
- **fix:** Narrow the rule: "Minarets and domes are fine in modern scenes and generic Islamic settings. Scenes set in the Prophet's ﷺ Madinah show no minaret, no dome, no city wall and no multi-storey towers: low mud-brick houses, palm groves, the masjid as an open courtyard with palm-trunk pillars and a frond roof." Paint the minarets out of the four files (the crescent tower in madinah.png matters most, because it sits on the mosque).
- **refuter:** A valid rule-challenge: the minaret with a crescent on the p3 Madinah painting is a historical error in an evidence-adjacent scene, and the current rule allows it.

### curriculum-designer-9 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L1 p24 s2 (all viewports)

### learning-scientist-12 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** course (MAP "The thread that ties the lessons" §1; L1 p24 → L2 p1)

l02 line 520).
- **why:** reading back one's own sentence and hearing the teacher restate the model is re-exposure, not retrieval; spaced

lessons back.

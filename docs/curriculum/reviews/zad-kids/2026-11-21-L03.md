## Blocker (1)

### subject-scholar-1 · confirmed · status: open
- **where:** L3 p6 s1–s5 (all viewports); also p6 teacher explain line 2
- **evidence:** `steps/p06.png` tiles 2–6: the stage tag under «لَا إِلَٰهَ» reads "no one else is worshipped"
(`src/curriculum/slides/lessons/l03.tsx:170`). Teacher explain: "The scholars explain la ilaha illallah as: nobody is
truly worshipped except Allah." The Arabic teacher note for the same page (`l03-teacher-ar.ts:62`) has the correct
formula: «لا معبود بحق إلا الله». The English drops «بحق».
- **why:** "No one else is worshipped" is false as a statement of fact (people do worship idols, stars, people) and is the exact
wording the books of creed warn against: without "by right/deservedly" the sentence either lies about the world or
implies that whatever is worshipped is Allah. The child-facing screen is the only place the meaning is spelled out, and it
spells it out wrong. Outcome 2 itself says "nobody deserves to be worshipped except Allah", so the slide contradicts the
lesson's own outcome.
- **fix:** tag → "no one else deserves worship"; teacher explain → "The scholars explain la ilaha illallah as: nobody deserves
to be worshipped except Allah (لا معبود بحق إلا الله)."
- **refuter:** `l03.tsx:170` at c7965cad renders the tag "no one else is worshipped" on every p6 step (sheet p06 tiles 2-6), a false creed statement that contradicts outcome 2; the English explain "nobody is truly worshipped" is acceptable ("truly" = بحق), so the fix is the tag.

### learning-scientist-1 · confirmed · status: open

journey' and 'none of us knew him' in the English line, then show the matching Arabic words".
- **refuter:** Sheet p03 tile 2 shows the English meaning and tiles 3–5 drop it while line 2 keeps referring to "two things"; p05 does the same, p04 keeps it, so the player is inconsistent and the child loses the only text they can read.

### subject-scholar-2 · judgement · status: open
- **where:** L1 p12 (items), p13 sort (cards 4 and 6), outcomes[1] (course)
- **evidence:** outcome 2 says "Knows the three levels of the deen" and the L01 brief says "the deen has three levels". But p12 shows three doors side by side, listed "Iman | Islam | Ihsan", and the p13 game treats them as three separate bins: "Telling the truth when nobody would find out" → ihsan only, "Doing wudu carefully, without rushing" → ihsan only. Wudu and truthfulness are deeds, so they fit "Islam: what I do" as well.
- **why:** in Ahl al-Sunnah teaching the hadith's three answers are *maratib* (levels, ranks) of one deen. They nest: Ihsan is the way you do the deeds of Islam with the faith of Iman. It is not a third pile of separate deeds. A game that marks "telling the truth" wrong when it goes through the Islam door teaches the categories as exclusive, and that is false. It also clashes with the lesson's own word "levels". The p12 order (Iman first) does not follow the order of the hadith (Islam, Iman, Ihsan) that pages 6–9 just taught, so the retell on the same page has two orders to choose from.
- **fix:** p12 items in hadith order and phrased as levels: "Islam: what I do", "Iman: what I believe", "Ihsan: doing it my very best, because Allah sees me". Sort game: make the Ihsan cards the *same* deed done two ways, so the bin tests the manner and not the deed: "Praying" → Islam, "Praying slowly, thinking Allah sees me" → Ihsan; "Fasting in Ramadan" → Islam, "Fasting even when nobody would know if I ate" → Ihsan. Add one line to p13 explain: "Ihsan is not a different deed; it is any deed done at its best because Allah sees you."
- **refuter:** p13 bins the deeds "Telling the truth…" and "Doing wudu carefully…" as Ihsan only, which teaches the levels as exclusive piles against the lesson's own word "levels"; p12 items run Iman, Islam, Ihsan after p6–p9 taught Islam, Iman, Ihsan.

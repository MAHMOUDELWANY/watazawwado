# English · Kids · Lesson 1 brief: The Snake Sound

Status: built 2026-10-26 as a prototype, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. Kids look, literacy cast
(Lina, Sam).

- **Driving question:** a snake in the garden says "sss": what does that sound look like on paper?
- **Sounds:** /s/ /a/ /t/ (sounds, not names), each letter written from its starting dot.
- **Wow:** blending: the three sound tiles slide together until they become "sat"; then a-t → "at".
- **Practice:** find /s/ on the fence; sort six words by their first sound; read "at" and "sat" alone.

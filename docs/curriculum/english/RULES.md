# English · binding rules (all tracks)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-subjects-curricula-design.md` §4.7 and §5. `docs/curriculum/RULES.md` wins.

1. **Goal-based tracks:** Kids (phonics, then reading), General (English File spine), Exams (IELTS), Work. Placement asks
   the goal; the goal decides which lessons are core.
2. **Kids phonics order:** synthetic phonics, sounds before letter names, taught in the Jolly-style groups (s a t i p n
   first, so a child blends real words from Lesson 1). *Oxford Phonics World* 1–2 set the vocabulary fields and the
   letter-shape practice; the *Jolly Phonics* readers are the decodable stories. Scope only: never their pictures,
   chants or exercises.
3. **Every lesson ends with reading real words** the child can decode with the sounds taught so far, nothing more.
4. **Sounds, not names:** /s/ not "ess". Single sounds are recorded by a teacher (the clone is unreliable on lone
   phonemes); words and sentences use the "mahmoud" English clone.
5. Neutral, warm content; Muslim names and family life are welcome, religious teaching is not the subject.
6. Every new word goes in `vocab` (the spaced-repetition deck); Arabic teacher notes; `reviewed: false` until approved.

# Lesson 1 — The Visitor in White

Zad Essentials · Kids (6–11) · Part 1 · ~45 min (13 pages; short version stops after page 11)
Status: **A3 done (2026-10-16).** Final copy lives in `src/curriculum/content/zad-kids/l01-the-visitor-in-white.ts` (`reviewed: false` until the owner approves it in the player). The table below is the plan it was written from.

## Outcomes (what the teacher checks, mostly through the game)

1. Retells the story in three sentences: a stranger came, asked three questions, it was Jibril.
2. Knows the deen has three levels: **Islam** (what my body does), **Iman** (what my heart believes),
   **Ihsan** (doing my best because Allah sees me).
3. Sorts at least 5 of 6 example deeds into the right "door".
4. Behaviour: sits close and listens well when learning, and asks good questions (the Visitor's manners).

## Rational step (before the text)

"Why would a teacher ask a question she already knows the answer to?" → so the whole class learns. That is exactly
what the Visitor did.

## Visual & motion notes

- Madinah scene in warm dawn colours; the Prophet ﷺ and the Sahabah are never drawn (light, silhouettes of the
  masjid, the circle of sitting places); Jibril shown only as the white-clothed stranger from behind/at a distance, no face.
- Doors animate open one by one on page 10; the same door icons return in every later lesson.
- Sound effects only: soft page turn, door creak, a gentle chime on the reveal and on correct game answers.

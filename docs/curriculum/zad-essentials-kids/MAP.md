# Zad Essentials · Kids (6–11) — Unit Map

Status: **approved by the owner 2026-10-16** (with his reorder). Rules: `../PLAN.md` §3, §3a. Fiqh: Shafi'i.
Each lesson ≈ 45 min, paged (teacher can finish in 30 or stretch to 60). Ayat verified with the quran.com connector;
hadith marked ✔ are verified on Dorar, others are "to verify" before their lesson brief.

## Spine and threads (what connects everything)

- **Spine:** the hadith of Jibril (Muslim 8 ✔). Lesson 1 tells it; the three questions become **three doors**:
  Iman (Part 1) → Islam in practice (Parts 2–4) → Ihsan (Part 5).
- **Story world [Proposed]:** siblings **Yusuf (9)** and **Maryam (7)** live in a Western city with their grandfather
  **Jiddo**, who answers their questions with stories, and their cat **Mishmish**. Real-life scenes: school, park,
  football, birthdays, the supermarket, screen time, a new kid in class.
- **The Zad backpack [Proposed]:** Jiddo gives them an old travel backpack. Each lesson packs one "provision" (the
  idea + the deed); it fills as the student's puzzle fills. Lesson 37 opens it all.
- **Bridges:** each lesson ends with a cliffhanger answered in the next; each part ends with a question that opens
  the next part.

## Game checkpoint (end of every lesson)

A short game (3–5 min) inside the lesson's last pages. It feels like play but checks the lesson's outcomes; results
feed the teacher's report as suggested strengths / things to reinforce (stealth assessment). Game types we rotate:
sort into boxes/doors, put steps in order, spot the mistake (in Yusuf's wudu, Maryam's salah…), match pairs (memory
cards), "help the character choose", true/false swipe, find the hidden clue in a scene, fill the missing word.

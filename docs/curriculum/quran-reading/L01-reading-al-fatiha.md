# Quran Reading · Lesson 1 brief: Reading Al-Fatiha from the Page

Status: built 2026-10-26 as a prototype, draft (`reviewed: false`), for the owner's review. One shared 12+ version on the
Adults stage (a Kids look can follow, as for Tajweed).

- **Driving question:** you say Al-Fatiha seventeen times a day; can you read it from the page, word by word?
- **The method of the course:** listen (the reciter, once), follow (a gold line sweeps under the ayah, right to left),
  read (alone; the teacher marks each ayah smooth, paused or helped).
- **Reading focus:** the small alif (ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ، ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ، مَـٰلِكِ، ٱلصِّرَٰطَ), the still waw of يَوْمِ, the shadda of
  إِيَّاكَ, starting on hamzat al-wasl (ٱهْدِنَا), the madd sign of ٱلضَّآلِّينَ.
- **Review:** eight words of the surah, with or without a small alif (checked against the fetched text).
- **Text and audio:** Tanzil Uthmani Hafs 1:1–7 via the Quran connector (2026-10-26), unchanged; Husary (muallim)
  streamed per ayah. No clone audio in this lesson. English meanings of the ayat are ours, for the owner's review.
- **For the owner to check:** the age (12+ now; the catalog had planned 6+), and whether teacher marks should be saved to
  the student map (site work, later).

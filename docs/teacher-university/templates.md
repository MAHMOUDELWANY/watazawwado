## 5. Homework set-up
Goal and why: ___ What exactly to do: ___ Format and length: ___ Tip: ___ Due: ___ Feedback I will give: ___

Homework tracker (shared with the learner)
| Date | Task | Done? | My feedback | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Start-of-lesson check-in: How was it (easy, hard, just right)? What did you do? Fix one thing together. Next time we focus on ___.
Learner reflection: What did you enjoy? What was hard? What did you use? How do you feel about it? What do you want next?

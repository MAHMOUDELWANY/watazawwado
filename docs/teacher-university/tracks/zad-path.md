# Track: Zad Path (Zad Essentials and Seerah; Kids, Teens, Adults)

DRAFT. Needs review before use. How the modules apply; content and sequence stay in the curriculum plan.

| Module | What it means here |
|---|---|
| 1 Know your learner | Prior knowledge from home or Sunday school; attention span; the questions they already carry. Adults: goals and time. Kids: parent plus child. |
| 2 Plan the lesson | Objective example (adults, Seerah): "Place the three events in order and explain why each mattered." Plan: text, prediction question, discussion. |
| 3 Frameworks | Text-based fits Seerah and hadith: orient, read, gist, language focus, discussion. TTT works for etiquette (ask first, teach the gap). |
| 4 Make meaning clear | Build a concept (sabr, ihsan) with a situation, then CCQs that separate it from look-alikes. |
| 5 Rules and patterns | Short situation, noticing questions, then the principle, then use it in a new situation. Rulings follow the curriculum plan. |
| 6 Reading | Text first, never a paraphrase before it. Gist first, a few words, a detail question, then a "what would you do" task. |
| 7 Speaking and writing | "Explain it to a younger child", a debate for teens, a short reflection or a letter using a model. |
| 8 Correction | Correct wrong understanding with a question, never ridicule. Kids: effort first, on the spot, playful. |
| 9 Sound | Reciting dua and adhkar correctly: same steps as Tajweed, lighter. |
| 10 Revision and homework | Retell last week from another person's point of view; a short story card as homework; family conversation tasks. |
| 11 Beliefs, L1 | Key texts stay in Arabic with clear explanation. |
| 12 Growth | Peer observation across age bands (a Kids teacher watches a Teens lesson and the reverse). |

## Age notes
- **Kids:** short blocks, movement, story, praise; lessons are closer to play. Delayed correction rarely works.
- **Teens:** real questions, room to disagree safely, relevance to their world.
- **Adults:** reasons and goals first, respect for their time and experience, depth when they ask.

## Standing rules

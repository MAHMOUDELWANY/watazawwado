# Track: Quran (reading, Tajweed, memorization and revision)

DRAFT. Needs review by our Quran teachers before use. This track only says how the teaching-craft modules apply. It does not define any ruling, level or method of the Quran subjects; those live in the curriculum plan (`docs/curriculum/`).

| Module | What it means here |
|---|---|
| 1 Know your learner | Reading level, which sounds are hard, why they want it (prayer, family, exam), time at home, who listens at home. For children: parent and child separately. |
| 2 Plan the lesson | An objective names the lines and the skill: "recite lines 1 to 5 with the ghunnah in every place it appears". Plan model, isolated drill, connected reading. |
| 3 Frameworks | TTT is natural: listen to the learner recite first, find the real problem, teach, listen again. PPP works for a single rule. |
| 4 Make meaning clear | Meaning, rule, recitation. CCQs about where a sound stops and for how long. |
| 5 Rules and patterns | Let the ear find the pattern before naming the rule. Isolation, then a few ayat, then free recitation. |
| 6 Listening | Listening to a model reciter, then to the learner's own recording. Gist: what is this passage about. |
| 7 Speaking | Recitation tasks with a repeat after feedback. |
| 8 Correction | Sound errors are often corrected on the spot; protect flow in long readings by noting, then returning. In memorised text, correct and re-recite immediately. |
| 9 Sound | The method for makharij and sifat: where it is made, how air and voice behave, model, imitate, drill, apply. |
| 10 Revision | The new / recent / older structure of revision is the same logic as spaced repetition. Plan all three each lesson. |
| 11 Beliefs, L1 | Text stays in Arabic; meaning is explained in the learner's language. Rulings follow the curriculum, not personal taste. |
| 12 Growth | Peer observation of a recitation lesson, with one focus. Recorded lesson with consent. |

## Standing rules
- The text is shown before any paraphrase. A teacher never replaces the text with a summary.
- Corrections are specific and kind. Name the physical thing ("tongue position", "how long you held it"), not "wrong".
- Anything that is a ruling is decided by the curriculum and senior teachers.

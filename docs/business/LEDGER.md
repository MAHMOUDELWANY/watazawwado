## Payment fees (Stripe, account in Poland, checked 2026-11-17)

Standard EEA cards 1.5% + 1 zł, UK cards 2.5% + 1 zł, international cards 3.25% + 1 zł, plus 2% when currency conversion is needed. With mostly US/UK families that is about 5% per payment. Settling in USD (a USD bank account in Stripe) would remove the 2%; to check.

## Money rules (advice agreed as defaults, 2026-11-17)

- The owner's teacher pay is counted like any teacher's, separate from the platform's profit.
- Keep a reserve of 3 months of fixed costs (~$450) before any paid ads.
- After that, 20–30% of platform net goes to marketing; the rest to reserve and scholarships.
- No debt: big spending (server, ads, staff) waits until revenue covers it for 3 months.
- Try any new tool free first; review every subscription every 3 months.

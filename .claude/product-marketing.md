# Product Marketing Context: Watazawwado Academy

Document version: 1.0 (2026-10-19)
Shared context for the marketing and social-media skills. Binding sources win over this file: root `RULES.md`,
`docs/marketing/RULES.md`, `docs/marketing/PLAN.md`. Keep this file a summary; update it when they change.

## 1. Product overview
- One line: 1-on-1 online Quran, Islamic studies and Arabic for Western Muslim families, with your own teacher and a
  curriculum you can see.
- Category people search: "online Quran classes for kids", "online Quran teacher", "learn Tajweed online", "learn
  Arabic online", "Egyptian Arabic tutor".
- Type: online academy (service), team of male and female teachers. Not a marketplace.
- Subjects: Quran Reading, Tajweed, Memorization & Revision ($9/h); the Zad Path (our curriculum: Zad Essentials and
  Seerah, Kids 6–11 / Teens 12–17 / Adults) ($9/h); Fusha Arabic, Egyptian Arabic, English (from $13/h). Lessons 30, 45
  or 60 min, price proportional; packages of 4/8/12 lessons with modest discounts; family 5% for siblings. Free trial:
  one per new student, 30 min.

## 2. Target audience
- Parents (mostly mothers, 28–45) in US/UK/Canada/Australia/Europe with children 6–14; Arab diaspora parents whose
  children are losing Arabic; adult learners and reverts.
- Jobs: "give my child a consistent, kind, qualified teacher"; "make Quran something my child looks forward to";
  "let me see progress"; "keep my child's Arabic alive"; "teach me to read the Mushaf properly".

## 4. Pains
- Classes with no plan and no visible progress; bored children; teachers who change; times in a foreign time zone;
  unclear monthly plans; safety worries (who is with my child online?).

## 5. Competitive landscape
- Direct: Egypt/Pakistan-based online Quran academies (monthly plans ~$16–40, 2 sessions a week); tutor marketplaces.
- Secondary: free YouTube channels and apps (Quran/Arabic content, no teacher).
- Indirect: the local masjid weekend school, a family member teaching.
- Never name competitors in public content.

## 6. Differentiation
Own animated curriculum with games and a teacher report after every class; placement test with a printable report;
your own teacher (kept, not swapped); clear hourly prices; times in your time zone; card/PayPal via Stripe; EU consumer
rights; male and female teachers; balanced, moderate Azhari foundation; Egyptian Arabic for heritage kids.

## 7. Objections
- "Is it safe for my child?" → Zoom waiting room, teacher hosts, you choose the teacher, the parent account
  manages the child's lessons and sees the teacher reports.
- "Why pay $9/h when others charge $30/month?" → compare per-minute; then the curriculum, the report, the kept teacher.
- "Will my child stay interested?" → free trial; games and stories in the lesson.
- Anti-personas: anyone wanting group classes, certificates/ijazah promises, or fatwas.

## 8. Switching
Push: boredom, no progress, teacher changes. Pull: visible curriculum, real teachers on video, clear prices.
Habit: "the current teacher is fine". Anxiety: wasting money, a stranger with my child → free trial, no contract.

## 9. Voice
The academy speaks ("we"). Warm, wise, family feeling, never salesy, short. Arabic: simple Fusha with a warm Egyptian
touch. No hype, no fake proof, no fake urgency. Full rules: `docs/marketing/RULES.md`.

## 10. Proof available
None public yet (launched Oct 2026). Do not invent any. Teachers' backgrounds only as on the About page.

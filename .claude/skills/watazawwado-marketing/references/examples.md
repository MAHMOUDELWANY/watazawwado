# Good and bad examples

| Bad | Why it fails | Good | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Expert, certified Quran teachers for your kids!" | Any academy; unprovable; hype | "Quick question: who's teaching your child the Quran? Not the academy. The person." | The enemy as a question a mother can answer |
| "We provide detailed progress reports." | Information, a feature | "Tuesday, 6:40 pm, your phone buzzes: 'Yusuf nailed the qalqalah today.'" | A scene; the promise "we message you first" |
| "Our holistic approach nurtures a lifelong love of Islam." | Abstract; outcome promise | "Same teacher. Same Tuesday. Six months later he still asks about your cat." | Concrete; shows "a mentor who stays" |
| "Join the best online Quran academy today! Limited spots!" | Hype, fake urgency, two claims | "Wave 1 is 20 learners. Pack your zaad and we'll tell you when it opens." | True scarcity, one CTA |
| "Ustadh Mahmoud will transform your child." | Centres one person; outcome promise | "Every teacher here works the same way: they get to know your child first." | Academy-led; an action we control |
| "Unlike X Academy, we don't swap teachers." | Names a competitor | "If the teacher keeps changing, every new one starts from zero. Here, your teacher stays." | Attacks the practice |
| "Weekend schools fail our kids." | Attacks the masjid; loses trust | "Alongside the masjid: one teacher who knows your child, from home." | Complement, not rival |
| "You've drifted from Allah. Time to repent." | Lecture; the exact fear of young adults | "Drifted? Start where you are. One letter. No lectures." | Meets them where they are |
| "Our parents love us! ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐" | Invented social proof | A real review, quoted exactly, with permission, labelled who wrote it | Credible |
| "Memorise Juz 'Amma in 30 days!" | Outcome promise | "Ten minutes a day, one short surah, checked by your teacher every week." | What we do, not what they'll become |

## A Short that passes the eight questions
"The stranger" (parents, pillar 1, rung 2): Hook "Who's teaching your child the Quran? Not the academy. The person."
→ "Most parents I taught couldn't tell me." → "When the teacher doesn't know your child, kids switch off." → "Here,
you choose the teacher and meet them first. And they stay." → "Nobody should learn their deen from a stranger. Pack
your zaad." CTA: comment ZAAD. KPI: comments with ZAAD, waitlist "my child".

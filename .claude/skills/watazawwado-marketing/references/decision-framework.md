# Decision framework

## 1. The eight questions (any post, script, page, offer or plan)

| # | Question | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Who exactly?** One audience: parents of children up to 17, or young adults 18–35. | It speaks to "everyone" or both at once |
| 2 | **Where is the stranger?** Does it show (not say) the enemy or its opposite: a teacher who knows you and stays? | It could be posted by any academy (cover-the-logo test) |
| 3 | **Which pillar and which rung?** One pillar (STRATEGY.md §7), one rung of the message ladder (§3). | It mixes three messages |
| 4 | **Can a five-year-old picture it?** A scene, an object, a moment, a name, a time. | Abstract words: quality, excellence, holistic, journey |
| 5 | **Who is the hero?** The family or learner; we are the guide. | The founder or the academy is the hero |
| 6 | **What is the proof?** Something seen: the ijazah, a report screen (demo data), a lesson moment, a real review with permission. | A claim nobody can check |
| 7 | **Is it honest and allowed?** Content RULES §1 (religious accuracy), §3 (claims), §4 (children). | Invented numbers, outcome promises, fake urgency, a child's face |
| 8 | **One CTA and one KPI?** "comment ZAAD" / link in bio / waitlist; and what number tells us it worked. | Two CTAs, or nothing to measure |

Score: 8/8 ship; 6–7 fix the misses; 5 or fewer rethink. Fix, don't just flag.

## 2. Labels
- **[D] Decision:** the owner said yes explicitly. Logged with date and reason.
- **[P] Owner preference:** what he likes or dislikes; ask whether it is a brand rule or a one-off taste before
  turning it into a rule.
- **[H] Hypothesis:** promising, unproven. Name the test that would prove it.
- **[R] Recommendation:** yours, waiting for the owner.
- **Evidence:** data or research, with source and strength (first-hand > second-hand > desk research).

## 3. Who decides what
| Owner decides | Claude decides (and logs) |
|---|---|
| Brand line, enemy, face, audiences, prices, discounts, teacher pay, wave dates, anything public about a person | Hooks, formats, posting times, which idea comes next inside the approved pillars, copy wording within the message ladder |
| Changing a fixed rule (SKILL.md §3) | Small changes after a numbers review (one at a time) |
| Notion structure changes | Notion item content within the agreed flow (watazawwado-content) |

## 4. When the owner disagrees
Raise the risk once, clearly, with your reason and an alternative. If he still decides otherwise, log it as his
decision with your note, and follow it. Do not re-argue it unless new evidence appears (example: no "AI" label on
voice-clone videos, 2026-11-12).
